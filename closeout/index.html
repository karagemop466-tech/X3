Read this first

  • Week 10 was incomplete in every tree of this repository. The canonical feed on main labels 13 of 14 games SCHEDULED; they finished on 2026-11-16. This module closes them out and re-reconciles the whole season (150 games) against the standings page read on 2026-11-17T22:49Z.
  • The one pre-kickoff board the project had has now been graded. The prior session published fair lines on 2026-11-14 for 13 Week-10 games; the margin forecast beat 'predict zero' (9.937 vs 13.538), the cover probability did not beat a coin flip (Brier 0.2516 vs 0.25), and the naive Week-9 favourite picked the same winners as the model (0.8462 vs 0.8462).
  • A new board is frozen for Week 11 (2026-11-17T23:05:00Z, first kickoff Thu 2026-11-19 8:15 PM ET) with the model version, every input and every parameter printed on the row. That is the project's first genuinely pre-registered test, and it cannot be edited into a backtest without the commit history showing it.
  • Nothing is promoted, nothing is staked, no ROI is published. There are zero market prices in the repository; the difference columns exist and are empty by policy, not by omission.
  • 12 irregularities are flagged, none blocking, including one that would have silently dropped ten Rams games from any join (CL-F-04).
  • Two Game Books, not one. The second read settles a live argument in this repository: Baltimore's starting quarterback on 2026-11-16 was J.Brissett, with K.Slovis inactive and J.Fagnano dressed but not used - and no Jackson or Huntley anywhere in the document. It also proves the inactive blocks are complete (48 dressed + 6 not active = 54), which is what makes an absence readable as a roster fact.
