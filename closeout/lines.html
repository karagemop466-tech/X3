Least-squares (Massey) ratings with a sum-to-zero constraint, solved exactly rather than iterated, fitted on 150 games whose dates all precede the freeze. Home advantage is fitted, not assumed: -0.16 points, and it is set to 0 for neutral-site games in both the fit and the forecast.

Why the numbers look aggressive: this model prices on season-long points differential with no shrinkage (the ablation on the luck page shows what shrinking does). Under-dispersion is the honest diagnosis - the SD of its margins is 8.61 against a realised 13.47.
