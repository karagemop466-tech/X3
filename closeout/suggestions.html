How to read the queue

  • Unblocks the most: SG-C-01 (a price source) and SG-C-02 (thirteen more Game Books). Everything else is measurement plumbing.
  • Cheapest real protection: SG-C-07 - relabel every in-sample figure, which stops the site overselling itself at no cost.
  • The structural fix: SG-C-08, consolidation; this module is the fourteenth tree, and adding a fifteenth is not the answer.
  • Do not do: publish parlays or prop combos (SG-C-12) - the joint evidence does not exist and the measured side/over dependence spans zero.
