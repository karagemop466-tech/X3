Line-by-line verification log mapping each data row to its official source and status.

Verification method

  • Each data row was fetched from the official source listed and checked against the page content.
  • Only values present on the official page were recorded; nothing was inferred or filled in.
  • Where no official source exists (betting lines, correlations, leaderboard performance), the value is left blank and flagged unverified.
  • Irregularities in fetching or labeling are recorded on the Flagged for Review page with their source links.
