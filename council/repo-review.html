Irregularities found while reviewing

These are the findings that came out of reading the trees rather than running their gates. Each is also on the flagged page with its evidence.

  • Fifteen overlapping trees. The same unanswered question appears under different IDs on different trees, which is how the same gap gets reported as new work repeatedly. See S13.
  • A false blocked-source claim. The project records that nfl.com cannot be reached from the authoring environment and cites it as the reason tables are blank. It can be reached. See C-04.
  • A correlation that does not reproduce. See C-01 and the recomputation on the verification page.
  • Empty market columns presented as pending. They are not pending; no official source publishes the values. See C-02.
  • Rows with neither a link nor a flag. A prior cross-tree audit counted 658 across the older trees. See C-10.
