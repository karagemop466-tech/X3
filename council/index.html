The one decision that unblocks the rest

No NFL or club page publishes betting prices. That single fact is why the fair-versus-market difference, closing-line value, ROI and the leaderboard are all blank across every tree in this repository. It is a data-rights decision rather than an engineering task, and it is suggestion S01 on the suggestions page.
