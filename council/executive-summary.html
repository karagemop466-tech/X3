Executive summary

This is a decision surface, not a betting site. It states the eight requirements of the brief as acceptance tests, reports exactly which ones the repository meets today, and lists what must be decided before any of them can be. Every number below was either read from an official NFL page on 2026-11-14 or is left blank and marked UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred.

Verified and solid

The results foundation is real. 137 completed games are logged with a per-row official source URL, the 32 club standings reconcile, and the 345 row Week 10 injury report was spot-checked 50 rows against the official page with zero mismatches. This session read nfl.com directly; the long-standing note that it cannot be reached is wrong.

The blocking gap

No market price exists anywhere in the repository, and no NFL or club page publishes one. That single fact is why the fair-versus-market difference, closing-line value, ROI and the leaderboard are all blank. It is a data-rights decision, not an engineering task, and it is the first thing to resolve.

A confirmed defect, and its correction

One published correlation did not reproduce from the data it cites: r(game total, |margin|) was published as -0.161 when the correct value is -0.0157, and -0.161 is in fact the signed-margin statistic. Its written reading had the sign backwards too. The corrected pairs are now published separately in origin/, with n and a 95% interval each; a stale copy carrying the wrong label is still on the desk tree. The pipeline was always sound - the label and the prose were not.

What is genuinely missing

Player-prop history, a settlement rule, a dated depth chart and price data. Three of those four need a decision or a purchase; none can be fetched. The moneyline and the demotion gate were on this list at the start of the session and are no longer, because another session's module merged while this page was being written.

The honest read on skill

Zero bets have been graded, so no strategy on any tree has demonstrated skill, and none has been shown to lack it. 3 of 8 requirements are met today and the rest are partly met, with the same blocker behind almost all of them. That is not a failure of analysis; it is the correct state of a project that has not yet bought its price data. The origination machinery - side, total, moneyline, fair line, gates, demotion - is built and waiting.
