S01 Market data P0 - blocking

Status. PROPOSED - not started

Proposal. Decide the price source before building more on top of it. Three paths: (a) buy a historical odds licence and ingest it in CI; (b) run the GitHub Actions runner on a schedule to snapshot one public book's board at a fixed time each day and retain only what is needed for closing-line value; (c) declare prices out of scope and re-scope the site to model accuracy against results, dropping every ROI and leaderboard claim.

Evidence. Every market column in the repository is empty (V11-V14). Path (c) is the only one that costs nothing and it is also the only one that is honest today.

Unblocks. R1, R4, R5, R8, Q01, Q02, Q03, Q18

Effort. Path (c): an hour. Paths (a) and (b): a procurement or legal step plus a pipeline.

Risk. Path (b) risks a terms-of-use problem and a single-book bias. Path (a) costs money. Path (c) removes the competition half of the brief.

Decision. PROPOSED - awaiting your pick  |  Source. UNVERIFIED - decision, not a fetch

S02 Settlement P0 - blocking

Status. PROPOSED - not started

Proposal. Write the settlement rule down before any bet is graded: how a side settles on a tie, how a total settles on an exact landing, whether overtime counts, and what a push does to the record. One page, versioned, linked from every leaderboard row.

Unblocks. R2, R5, Q04

Effort. Small. It is a document, not a system.

Risk. Low, but if it is written after the first bet is graded the early record becomes unfixable.

S04 Governance P1 - high

Status. IMPLEMENTED by origin/ (PR #47) - gate D-1 with five triggers plus D-2 re-promotion. Still open: automating enforcement.

Proposal. Write the demotion gate as numbers with an owner and an enforcement point. A concrete default: return to paper if the rolling 200-bet mean closing-line value is negative, or if the 95% CI on ROI contains zero over the last 200 graded bets, or immediately on any data-provenance failure. Log every demotion with its trigger.

Evidence. Promotion gates G1-G8 exist; no demotion rule exists anywhere (R8).

Unblocks. R8, Q13, Q14

Effort. Small to write, larger to automate.

Risk. Thresholds chosen after seeing results are fitted to the past. Choose them now, before there is anything to demote.

Decision. PROPOSED - awaiting your pick  |  Source. UNVERIFIED - decision, not a fetch

S05 Leaderboard P1 - high

Status. IMPLEMENTED by origin/ (PR #47) - signal and staking are separate blocks on every row; the signal half is populated, the staking half is blocked.

Proposal. Split the leaderboard into two boards from the start: a signal board scored on stake-independent mean closing-line value per bet with n and a 95% CI, and a staking board scored on ROI, units and drawdown. Rank them separately and never blend them into one number.

Evidence. The design is described on two trees; neither board has a row (R5).

Unblocks. R5, Q11

Effort. Small once R1 closes. Nothing to build until then.

Risk. A blended ranking is exactly the failure the requirement is written to prevent.

Decision. PROPOSED - awaiting your pick  |  Source. UNVERIFIED - design, no graded bets exist

How to read the priorities

  • P0 - blocking. Nothing downstream can be finished until this is decided.
  • P1 - high. Large effect on whether the project can make a skill claim at all.
  • P2 - medium. Real improvement, not on the critical path.
  • P3 - low. Worth doing, safe to defer.

Priorities are this session's recommendation, not a decision. Change them; the point of the page is that the choice is made before the work starts.
