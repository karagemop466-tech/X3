Questions, answers and information gaps

What we know, what we do not, what each gap blocks, and how to close it. Every 'known' cell links the page it was read from; every 'missing' cell names the specific absent fact rather than a category.

Every question below is in one of three states. Known is something this repository has verified against an official page, with the link. Missing is the specific thing that is not known. Suggestion is the concrete path that would close the gap - including, where the answer cannot be fetched, the decision that has to be made instead.

A question whose answer cannot be bought, fetched or computed is not listed as a question. It is listed as a decision on the suggestions page.
