Choosing a path

PathSequenceWhat you getWhat you do not get
A - Evidence first (recommended)S-01 archive policy -> S-03 game log -> S-02 prices -> calibrate (S-09) -> gates fire -> leaderboard populatesa model whose parameters are measured, a leaderboard that means something, and a market-relative verdict on our own linesnothing tradeable this month
B - Lines now, data laterS-01 -> publish the Week-10/11 boards on a weekly cadence -> log our own lines as the record -> S-02/S-03 when budget allowsa weekly decision aid immediately, plus a home-grown price/result archive built from our own published lines (which later becomes the backtest data)no CLV against a real market until prices arrive; our own lines cannot validate themselves
C - Signals firstS-04 preregistration (already adopted) -> add injury-shift and lineup-deficiency flags to the board -> manual record of bets you choose to place -> score against the ledgeractionable flags on the pages you already read, and an honest record from day onesmall samples for a long time; the min-sample gate will keep the board empty for seasons

Recommended

Path B, then A. Publish the weekly board now - it is already built and its inputs are verified - and start a self-owned line record from this week, because those rows are the one asset that appreciates with time and costs nothing but a weekly run. Then buy the game log and the price archive, which is what converts a nice-looking model into a validated one. Do not fund a leaderboard or set prize amounts before the gates pass: with zero settled bets, a funded competition is a coin flip with a prize.
