The three that matter most

  1. R-07, the minimum sample gate. The published 300-bet minimum would admit strategies indistinguishable from luck. It is corrected to about 2,272 bets for a 55% edge, derived from exact power rather than opinion.
  2. R-08, the model's largest adjustment is unmeasured. The injury and positional costs move lines by up to 6.5 points with no derivation anywhere in the repository. They are honestly labelled as parameters, but the label understates how much of the board they decide.
  3. R-06, two depth captures disagree. Two files, both internally correct, giving different counts for the same week. Neither page is wrong and a reader comparing them will be confused, so the capture source needs labelling or consolidating.
