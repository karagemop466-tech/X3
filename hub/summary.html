The short version

Three things changed in this session, and one did not.

  1. A results feed now exists and is verified. 137 finals were re-summed per club and compared field by field with the official standings page: 192 of 192 values agree exactly across 32 clubs. Before this, backtests in this repository were not possible at all.
  2. The model was tested out of sample for the first time. On 89 games it had never seen, it beats predicting a zero margin: MAE 9.29 vs 10.21 points, with 56 of 88 sides correct (exact p=0.01375). That is a real signal about the score. It is not an edge over a price, and this site never claims one.
  3. The gates got arithmetic. The published minimum sample of 300 bets was too low by a factor of about 8: an exact power calculation puts a 55% edge at 2272 settled bets. A demotion gate now exists with six triggers, each backed by a probability rather than an opinion.

What did not change: there is still no market price anywhere in this repository. Every price cell is blank and flagged, so ROI, closing-line value and a populated leaderboard remain impossible. That is not a modelling gap, it is a missing purchase.
