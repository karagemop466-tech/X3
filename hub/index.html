X3 NFL decision desk

Collect, organise and analyse NFL evidence for originating our own lines, with every row tied to an official source and every gap published instead of filled.

Read this first

This repository is an evidence-first decision desk, not a tipster. Three things are true at once: the injury and standings data on this site is verified against official NFL pages; the model lines are derived arithmetic on that data using parameters that are stated in the open and are not yet calibrated; and anything that would require a betting market - prices, closing-line value, settled bets, ROI, a populated leaderboard - is blank and flagged, because no such data exists in this repo. Nothing here was inferred to fill a gap.
