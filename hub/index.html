X3 NFL decision desk

Collect, organise and analyse NFL evidence for originating our own lines, with every row tied to an official source and every gap published instead of filled.

Read this first

This repository is an evidence-first decision desk, not a tipster. Four things are true at once: the injury and standings data is verified against official NFL pages; the results are verified too, because 137 final scores reconcile to the official standings with 0 mismatches across 32 clubs; the model lines are derived arithmetic on that data with every parameter stated in the open; and anything that would require a betting market - a market line, a difference, closing-line value, ROI, a populated leaderboard - is blank and flagged, because NFL.com publishes no odds at all. Nothing here was inferred to fill a gap.
