Validation gates

The tests every strategy must pass before it may appear on a leaderboard or influence a stake.

Gates (10)

A strategy is a candidate until it passes every gate. The gates exist to make it expensive to be fooled: sample size, chronology, multiple-testing, intervals and exposure each remove a different way that luck masquerades as skill.
