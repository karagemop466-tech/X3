Luck decomposition

Separating what the points say from what the wins say, so that 'skill' has to clear a measured noise floor.

How much of a record is not skill

Pythagorean wins = g * PF^k/(PF^k+PA^k), k=2.37. Luck residual = actual wins - Pythagorean wins. R^2 of net points per game on win pct is the share of record variance the scoring differential already explains; the remainder is outcome noise.

Read carefully. 'Luck residual' is a statement about how the points and the wins disagree on one date - it is not proof that a team was unlucky, and it is not by itself a tradable signal. Its only legitimate use here is as a warning: a 7-2 record and a 5-4 record can describe the same team, so any leaderboard that rewards a short record is rewarding a coin.
