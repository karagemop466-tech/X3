Why this matters for parlays

Two legs priced at 0.55 each multiply to 0.3025 only if they are independent. If the true joint is 0.40 - which is normal for two outcomes decided by the same game script - a ticket sold at 0.3025 is priced 32% rich. The dependence term is therefore not a refinement of parlay pricing; it is the whole question. Note also that the two variables we could measure that are closest to 'form' are weakly tied: home win pct vs road win pct is r=0.3501, which is why 'how they play at home' is not portable evidence for a road game.
