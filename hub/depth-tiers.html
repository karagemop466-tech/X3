Depth of the replacement, not the fact of one

"A backup starts" is not a measurement. The useful question is which backup, and what the drop is: a 2nd-stringer replacing a starter is a different event from a 4th-stringer doing it. This page resolves each Out or Doubtful designation to the player's position on his club's own depth chart, and reports the tier of the man who steps in.

What this cannot tell you

  • Most clubs label their depth chart unofficial and few publish an as-of date, so the chart is evidence of a published ordering, not of who will actually start.
  • A player missing from a chart is not evidence he is not on the team; it is a capture limitation and is reported as one.
  • The official injury report gives status, never a depth position. Nothing here overrides the official status.
