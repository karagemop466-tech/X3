Leaderboard

A competition design that rewards strategies only after they beat a luck-adjusted baseline - and which is therefore empty today.

Signal and staking are two different columns

A strategy can look profitable because of when it was staked, and a good signal can be buried by bad staking. This leaderboard therefore scores the two separately, from different data, and never adds them into one number. The signal column is measured on outcomes with n and an interval; the staking column needs a price and is empty because no price exists. A strategy is ranked only when both columns exist - which today means none is.

The scoring contract

RuleConsequence
Signal and staking are scored in different columns from different data and are never combined into one number.
A strategy is leaderboard-eligible only when its signal is ESTABLISHED (whole CI above the baseline), its staking score exists with n, and the demotion gate is not firing.
A good staking run cannot promote a strategy whose signal is INCONCLUSIVE or FAILED; a good signal cannot be published with a staking number that does not exist.
Every number carries n. A row without n is not ranked.

Ranking rules

  1. A strategy is invisible to the leaderboard until G-1 through G-9 all return PASS.
  2. Ranking is by risk-adjusted return, not by win count; a 30-20 record is not evidence.
  3. Every published number carries its sample size and its interval; a row without n is not shown.
  4. Re-rating happens only on new weeks; a strategy that decays is demoted automatically.
  5. No real money, no payout and no bet placement exists in this repository.
