Ranking rules

  1. A strategy is invisible to the leaderboard until G-1 through G-9 all return PASS.
  2. Ranking is by risk-adjusted return, not by win count; a 30-20 record is not evidence.
  3. Every published number carries its sample size and its interval; a row without n is not shown.
  4. Re-rating happens only on new weeks; a strategy that decays is demoted automatically.
  5. No real money, no payout and no bet placement exists in this repository.
