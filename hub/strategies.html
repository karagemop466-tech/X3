Luck-removal programme

  • Separate variance from skill by measuring against prices, not outcomes alone (G-04, G-05).
  • Correct for the trials you ran, not just the strategy you kept (G-06, G-07).
  • Require out-of-sample and leave-best-out tests before promotion (G-08, G-10).
  • Cap the things that make luck look like skill: correlated legs, small samples, post-hoc thresholds (G-01, G-09).
  • Publish the residual of W-L% over net points as a luck index for clubs, and treat its mean reversion as the hypothesis (SIG-11).
