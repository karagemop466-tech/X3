Depth of the replacement, not the fact of one

Two absences at the same position are not the same event. This page resolves every official Out/Doubtful designation to the man who replaces him and to his rung on the club's chart, by walking down the chart from the unavailable player to the first name that is not also unavailable in the same report. Where that walk cannot be completed from an official source, the row is published UNVERIFIED with the reason - it is never filled with a plausible name.
