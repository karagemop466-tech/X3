Rule applied here

Every coefficient on this page is computed from officially sourced numbers only, and every value is printed with its n, a 95% interval and a permutation p-value. A coefficient whose interval includes zero is reported as not distinguishable from no relationship, not as a trend. The p-value comes from 20,000 shuffles with a fixed seed, so it is reproducible and does not assume normality.

What this means for parlays

Two legs may only be multiplied as independent if the joint is actually the product of the marginals. The measurable pieces of that question are on this page, and the answer for the two pairs that matter most in a same-game ticket is: no dependence is detectable on this sample. That is not the same as independence proven, and it does not extend to player props, which have no official archive here.

The corrections this page makes
