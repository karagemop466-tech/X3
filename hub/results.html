Verified results feed

Every final score in the repository, reconciled field by field against the official standings page. This is the layer that makes a backtest possible.

Why this page exists

Before this feed, no backtest in this repository could be computed, because there was no verified record of a final score. The feed is now the base layer everything else is measured against, and it is reconciled rather than trusted.

Reconciliation against the official standings

Every final was re-summed per club and compared with the official standings page. The test is deliberately mechanical: wins, losses, ties, points for, points against and games played must all reproduce exactly, or the feed is rejected.
