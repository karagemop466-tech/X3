Price snapshot store

Every market we originate gets a dated opening and closing observation in a committed, append-only store - and a blank market column, because no official price exists.

What this store is for

A backtest is only as good as the numbers it grades. This store gives every market we originate a dated opening and closing observation in a committed file, so a future backtest grades the number we actually published rather than a number refitted with hindsight. It deliberately does not contain a market price: that column is blank because no official source publishes one.

Open (unplayed game)our number fitted on every final available nowone capture, no second observation yet

Honest limits

There is one snapshot milestone so far, so every close in the store is a reconstructed close (our own model refitted), not a captured one. The movement figures above are therefore the movement of our own number, and are labelled that way wherever they appear. Turning this into a real open/close record needs a scheduled capture, which is suggestion S-17.
