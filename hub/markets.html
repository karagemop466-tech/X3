Originated markets: side, total, moneyline

Our own line for every unplayed game, originated in the order the arithmetic allows, with the fair line, the market line and the difference kept as separate columns.

The order is the requirement

Side first, then the total, then the moneyline. That order is not cosmetic: the moneyline is derived from the side model's margin distribution, so it cannot be priced before the side exists, and the total is fitted separately because borrowing the side's evidence for it would double-count the same games.

Origination order, step by step

StepMarketHow it is producedWhy in this order
1Side (handicap)margin model on final scores: home_margin = hfa + net[home] - net[away]the side is the strongest of the three and does not depend on the other two
2Totalseparate pace model on the same finals: total = base + pace[home] + pace[away]fitted second because it is the weaker model; side and total are only weakly related (measured r published on the correlation page), so the total cannot borrow the side's evidence
3MoneylineP(home win) = Phi(margin / sigma), sigma from walk-forward residualsa moneyline is a side with the handicap removed, so it inherits the side's error by construction and is never priced from an independent guess
