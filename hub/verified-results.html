Why this feed can be trusted enough to backtest on

A results feed that is not reconciled is a source of confident nonsense. Three independent checks run on every build: completeness (a final without both scores is dropped, never filled), reconciliation to the official standings through a different page transcribed by a different session, and a score-by-score comparison of a sample against a live read.

Why there is a second results page

A parallel session also publishes a results page at hub/results.html, built from its own capture of the same official week pages. Both reconcile to the same official standings with zero mismatches. They are kept side by side rather than merged or overwritten, and that duplication is recorded as flag F-27.

Limits, stated on the feed itself

  • One season to date. No prior-season results are in this repository, so no strategy can clear a three-season walk-forward yet (gate G-3).
  • The capture covers weeks 1-11 of the 2026 schedule only.
  • No prices are attached to any row. A result grades a bet; it cannot price one.
