Fetch boundary

Direct network access from the build environment is limited to github.com, api.github.com, codeload.github.com, registry.npmjs.org, pypi.org and files.pythonhosted.org. Pages on nfl.com and club domains are read through the agent's fetch tool, which returns the rendered document; the URLs above are the addresses to open manually to confirm any row. No value on this site was taken from a third-party aggregator.

Rows are read from official NFL.com and NFL club pages on 2026-11-14; values with no official source are left blank and flagged.
