The path this session recommends

  1. Decide the competition rules now, before any results exist (S-01). The rules are cheap today and impossible to justify later, because after the first winning run every rule change looks like sour grapes.
  2. Treat the price feed as the gating purchase (S-02). Nothing else on the site is blocked on analysis; everything is blocked on prices.
  3. Do not estimate the injury coefficients from one season (S-03). Wait for the data rather than fitting a coefficient to noise, and until then keep the adjustment flagged as uncalibrated.
  4. Get the legal question answered before publishing rewards (S-08). It is the only item here that no amount of data can resolve.
