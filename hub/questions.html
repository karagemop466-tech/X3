A-01Does an official, machine-readable NFL injury report exist, and what does it contain?Yes. The Week 10 2026 report published 345 rows across 28 clubs (the 4 on bye publish none), each with position, injury, practice participation and, for 160 of them, a game status of Out, Doubtful or Questionable.SRC-02
A-02Can the hub quote a market line or a prop price as fact?No. No official NFL page publishes lines or prop prices, so every market cell in this hub is blank and flagged rather than filled from an unverified third party.FLG-07
A-03Is there an official depth chart that identifies second, third or fourth string players?No. Only club self-published charts, labelled unofficial by the clubs, with coverage and dating gaps; string-depth columns are therefore blank here and availability is tracked from the injury report instead.FLG-08
A-04Can standings be used as a clean as-of feature?Yes, once stamped - and an assumption about the cut-off is exactly what produced a wrong flag here. This session first published 'the standings module lags the completed Thursday game', then disproved it by reconciling the page against an independent game log: the page covers every game through 2026-11-12 and simply prints no cut-off. The surviving rule is to record the last included game with the snapshot (hub/data/asof.json).SRC-01, SRC-17, VER-19
A-05Can luck be removed from NFL results?No, it can only be bounded and measured. The defensible programme is to pre-register a strategy, require an out-of-sample track record, then correct for selection bias and backtest overfitting.SRC-10, SRC-11
A-06Is the injury data already in this repo trustworthy?Verified row by row against the live official page: 344 of 345 rows identical on player and status, 1 practice-status drift in 24 hours. Game-status column reproduces exactly, 345/345.SRC-16, FLG-03

A-09What is the state of the site today?Fragmented: eight site trees from eight sessions, nine index files, four injury pages and five suggestions pages, and the root URL was a redirect. Consolidation is proposed as S-01 and the hub is the proposed canonical surface.FLG-01
