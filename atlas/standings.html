The standings page is missing one Week 10 game

Verified on 2026-11-17. The official league standings page has folded in 26 of the 28 clubs that played in Week 10. Four clubs (CHI, DEN, PHI, TB) were on bye and correctly show no change. The remaining two, Washington and the New York Giants, played each other on Thursday 12 November - Washington won 24-21 - and neither club's row has moved: Washington still reads 3-5-1 with 196 points for, and the Giants still read 5-3-1 with 203 points for, exactly as they did in the capture taken three days earlier on 2026-11-14. Their streak and last-5 columns also read 1W and 1L respectively in both captures.

Two consequences follow, and both matter. First, the standings imply 150 games played, while the official schedule page lists 14 Week 10 finals on top of the 137 the earlier capture implies - 151, not 150. Any check that reconciles a results feed to the standings on games played will fail by one game until the league corrects this. Second, every per-game rate for Washington and the Giants on this module is computed over nine games, not ten, because the published points-for and points-against are one game short. Those two clubs play in Week 11 (Cincinnati at Washington, Jacksonville at the Giants), so this affects the originated lines on the next page and is flagged there too.

This is stated as an observation about two official pages. The cause is not published, so it is not asserted here.
