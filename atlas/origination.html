Two model inputs are one game short

Washington and the Giants each play in Week 11, and each has only nine games in the official standings because the league has not credited their Thursday 12 November game to either club. Their points-per-game and points-allowed-per-game on this page are therefore computed over nine games rather than ten, so the Cincinnati-at-Washington and Jacksonville-at-Giants rows below carry a slightly noisier rate than the other eleven. The value is still the published value; it is not adjusted, because adjusting it would mean inventing the league's own correction. Flagged as F-16 and shown club by club on the standings page.

Why the market and difference columns stay blank

Every market cell on this page renders as UNVERIFIED - no official source. That is a deliberate state, not a formatting artefact. Publishing a market number taken from a bookmaker while the rest of the site is sourced only from the league would break the one rule that makes this desk useful: you can always tell which numbers are official.

The personnel ablation, not a penalty

The personnel variant applies a declared constant per Out designation. It is not a fitted penalty and it is not presented as a truth. It exists so that, once Week 11 results are in, the same model can be run with and without the personnel feature on a chronological holdout and the difference reported with an interval. The feature is drawn from the Week 10 report because no Week 11 designations have been published, which makes it stale by construction - that is precisely why the deliverable is the comparison, not the level.
