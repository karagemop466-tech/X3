How to read a designation

  • A designation is a level, not a signal. Out, Doubtful and Questionable are what the club published on a given day, and this desk will not convert them into a probability until the league's own designations have been joined to inactives over several seasons (requirement R-53, not started).
  • The signal is the change between two dated reads. That needs at least two captures a week, which is requirement R-54 and is not yet implemented - one capture is not a series.
  • A player absent from the report is not healthy. He may be on injured reserve: Malik Willis does not appear in the Week 10 report because Miami placed him on IR on 2026-11-11, not because he is available. nfl.com/injuries/league/2026/reg10
