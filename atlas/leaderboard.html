Leaderboard

The leaderboard is empty, and that is the honest state. It publishes a scoring design that luck cannot flatter, and it will populate only when strategies clear the gates on prospective results.

Signal and staking are two columns, never one

A good signal with reckless staking produces a big number for a while, and a bad signal with a lucky staking run produces the same thing. The leaderboard therefore scores them separately: the signal column is a proper score on the probability itself, and the staking column is reported beside it and never mixed in. A strategy with a negative signal score is not eligible however much money its staking plan made.

Proper scores with uncertainty

  • Brier skill, benchmark-relative. Skill is measured against a stated benchmark, not against zero, so a strategy is not credited for rediscovering that home teams exist.
  • Game-cluster lower bound. Bets inside one game are not independent. The interval is clustered by game, which widens it correctly.
  • Multiplicity adjustment. Eight strategies were tried, so the bar for the best of eight is higher than the bar for one pre-registered strategy.
  • Two control baselines on every run. Random picks and favourites-only are published beside every eligible strategy. If a strategy cannot beat a coin, it is not skill.
  • No ROI column at all. Ranking by season-long ROI is explicitly rejected: it cannot separate skill from variance at these sample sizes, and it rewards the largest stake.

The two-stage qualification

  1. Stage 1 - proper-score qualification. A strategy must show a positive skill score whose lower bound clears zero after clustering and multiplicity.
  2. Stage 2 - prospective CLV and ROI escrow. Only then does it enter a prospective sample where CLV and ROI are measured forward in time. Stage 2 is blocked today: it needs prices.

Rewards

No reward amount is published. A funded leaderboard is a contest and in some jurisdictions a regulated one, and nothing in this repository establishes a prize. Reward amounts stay notional until that question is answered on the suggestions page.
