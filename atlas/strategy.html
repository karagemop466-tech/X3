The two-sided field, and why it is frozen first

Every strategy carries both an upgrade trigger and a downgrade trigger, written before any result is known. A one-sided rule - "we will promote it if it wins" - can never be falsified, and a leaderboard that cannot be falsified is a marketing page. The numbers in the promotion column are project thresholds set by this desk, not figures published by the NFL.

Pre-registration: what counts

  1. The rule text, the sample window and the gates are committed to git before the evaluation window opens. The commit hash is the registration.
  2. A number graded on games that predate the commit is retrospective and must be labelled IN-SAMPLE. It cannot promote a strategy.
  3. Backtest numbers never appear on the leaderboard. The leaderboard shows prospective results only, so a strategy that was curve-fitted to history cannot buy its way to the top.

Combos and parlays: the joint-evidence gate

Multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence, and two legs in the same game are dependent by construction. A combo may be ranked only when a directly validated joint probability exists and the operator's void and push rules are recorded. Neither exists today, so no combo is ranked, and none will be until both do.
