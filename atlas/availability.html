Dated roster and availability events read from official NFL pages. This is the availability graph in its current, admittedly partial, form: one row per event, each with the date the league published it and the page it came from.

Why this page exists. Depth charts go stale. A club can list a player at a position weeks after he went on injured reserve and a replacement was traded in. Availability evidence has to be versioned against the last official roster event, otherwise "listed as the starter" quietly becomes a false statement. This register is the versioning spine.
