Price snapshot store and price policy

The price-snapshot store is defined and empty. The brief requires the price-data policy to be decided before any backtest is published, so the policy is published here first and the store opens only when a source class is approved.

Policy PDP-1, committed 2026-11-17. No backtest, no ROI figure, no CLV figure and no staking score may be published on this site until a price source class has been approved in writing on the suggestions page. Until then the leaderboard is a signal board and every market column renders as UNVERIFIED - no official source.

The ruling that matters

Opening and closing numbers are defined before they are measured: the open is the first reading taken after the market appears; the close is the last reading before kickoff. Both are appended with their timestamps. If that definition is not fixed in advance, closing-line value can be computed retroactively to favour whatever a strategy happens to have done, which is the exact failure the leaderboard design exists to prevent.
