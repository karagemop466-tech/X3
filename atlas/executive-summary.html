The four decisions that gate everything else

DecisionWhy it blocksIf it is not taken
Approve a price source classWithout any market price, fair-vs-market, CLV, ROI and every staking score are structurally impossible, not merely missing The board stays a signal board; no staking column can ever be filled
Approve an automated capture scheduleDesignations are a series, and one capture cannot produce a delta, so the injury signal can never be a surprise rather than a level Injury stays a stale level and the availability graph stays empty
Approve a depth-order source (chart or snap counts)"Second-string cornerback facing a number one receiver" cannot be computed without a verified depth rank for all 32 clubs The deficiency matrix stays blocked; it is not approximated
Rule on rewards and on storing raw rowsA funded leaderboard is a regulated contest in some jurisdictions, and league terms govern republication of row-level data Rewards stay notional and raw rows stay private
