Removal of luck

How much of a 2026 record is not skill. Pythagorean expectation turns points for and against into an expected win percentage; the gap between that and the actual record is the part of the record that points alone do not explain.

The panel

Expected win percentage uses the standard football exponent of 2.37, applied to the official points for and against of every club. The residual is actual wins minus expected wins. Across 32 clubs the residual has a standard deviation of 1.14 wins, so about two thirds of clubs sit within 1.14 wins of their points-based expectation and roughly one in twenty sits more than 2.29 wins away. A club that is two wins better than its points "should" be has usually been lucky in close games, not better.

The fit between expected and actual win percentage is r = 0.882 (r² = 0.779, n = 32), which means roughly 22% of the variation in win percentage is not explained by points scored and allowed at all.

What this does and does not license

  • It licenses refusing to promote a strategy because its record looked good. A two-win swing is inside the noise band of a ten-game sample.
  • It does not license adjusting a team rating by its residual. The residual is not a measurable skill; treating it as one is the oldest way to turn noise into a bet.
  • One season of one metric is one sample. The exponent is a convention, not a law, and the residual SD should be re-measured on prior seasons before it is used as a gate (R-45, R-53).
