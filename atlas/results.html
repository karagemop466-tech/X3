What a verified results row requires

  1. The score comes from an official NFL box score or schedule page, never from a live-score widget.
  2. The row is appended, never overwritten, and keeps the date it was read.
  3. A pending game is published as pending; a live score is never treated as a result.
  4. Each round is reconciled back to the official standings: wins, losses, ties, points for and points against must add up.
