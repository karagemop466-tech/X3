The three questions that gate everything

  1. Q-01 - a price source. Until this is answered, no CLV, no ROI, no staking column and no fair-versus-market difference can be published by this desk, ever. Everything else is work that can be done in parallel; this one cannot be routed around.
  2. Q-07 - a depth-order source. Until one route is verified for all 32 clubs, the deficiency matrix stays blocked. It is tempting to approximate; the brief forbids it, correctly.
  3. Q-11 - prior seasons. One season cannot satisfy a three-season gate. Either the archive is built or the gates are formally relaxed in writing, and relaxing a gate to fit available data is the decision this desk will not make on its own.
