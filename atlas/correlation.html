How to read these

  • Net points per game against win percentage is the strongest relationship here, which is close to circular: wins are made of points. It is published because it sets the ceiling every other correlation should be judged against.
  • The home-versus-road win percentage correlation is the portability check. If form were portable, a club's home record would predict its road record; the interval shows how weak that link is.
  • Turnover differential is the classic "lucky" quantity: it correlates with winning but is far less stable year to year than net points. This desk will not use it as a feature until its stability is measured across seasons (R-65, not started).

What is deliberately not correlated

No coefficient on this page mixes an official number with a modelled one, and no coefficient is computed on fewer than 26 observations. Relationships that need injury, depth or price data are left uncomputed until those datasets exist as dated captures rather than single reads.
