Depth and deficiency

The deficiency view the brief asks for - which second, third or fourth string player is replacing a starter - is the single largest gap on this site, and this page says so plainly rather than approximating it.

Blocked, and not approximated. A deficiency claim needs a verified depth rank for both sides of a matchup. This session verified venues, results, standings, team stats and injury rows from official pages. It did not verify a single depth rank, because the two candidate routes are both unresolved: club depth charts carry no as-of date, and no official snap-count page was read end to end this session. Publishing a "second-string cornerback" label from a jersey number, a roster order or a guess would be exactly the fabrication the brief forbids.

The two known parsing traps, recorded so nobody repeats them

  • Texans. Earlier sessions found Houston's depth published as an image. An image cannot be transcribed into a verified field; it needs an authorised structured source, not a human reading it.
  • Packers. Green Bay's chart prepends jersey numbers to player names. Stripping the number and keeping the raw string recovers the row; deriving depth order from the number is explicitly rejected (R-62).
