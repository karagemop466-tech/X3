Ablation, not penalty

The brief rejects subtracting a fixed number of points for a missing starter. The correct test is to run the identical model with and without the verified availability feature on chronological holdouts and report the difference with an interval. That is what the base and personnel variants on the origination page exist to do once Week 11 results are published. Until then the difference column is a declared specification, not a finding.

Train, validation, holdout

Not started. One season of 2026 cannot satisfy a three-season gate, and splitting a single season into three pieces is not the same thing as out-of-sample across seasons. Requirement R-47 stays open until prior seasons are archived (R-45).
