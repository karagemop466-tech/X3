How to use this page

  1. Read the priority column first. HIGH means other work cannot finish without it.
  2. Approve or reject each row. Rejecting is a valid outcome and cheaper than building the wrong thing.
  3. Only after a row is approved does it become a task with an owner and a file scope on the shared board. Nothing on this page has been started.

What this desk will not do without a decision

  • It will not invent a price to fill the market column.
  • It will not infer a depth rank from a jersey number, a roster order, experience or salary.
  • It will not relax a validation gate because the available sample is too small to clear it.
  • It will not publish a reward amount, and it will not republish raw league rows before the terms are read.
