Depth & replacements

String order, and what the official snap sheet says about it

The measured answer to 'model depth from playing time, not jersey order'

The official Game Book prints a Playtime Percentage sheet: every player who took a snap, by count and share. Against the club's own depth chart, for the one game where both exist:

19 of 23 slots agree (83%), 4 disagree. In four slots the club's first-listed player played fewer snaps than a backup. So the depth chart is a useful prior and not a reliable statement of who plays — which is the brief's point, measured rather than asserted.

Two more structural problems are published rather than papered over: 31 of 32 clubs print no as-of date (F-06), and the committed capture loses shared slots — Seattle lists two players in one rank-1 cell live, and the parser splits them into rank 1 and rank 2 (F-08).
