Price snapshots

Opening and closing rows for every originated market, with a hash

What a snapshot store has to do, and what this one cannot yet do

Requirement R-01 asks for opening and closing numbers for every market we originate. This desk stores 78 rows: 13 markets x 3 market types x 2 phases, each with a capture id (SNAP-2026-11-15-001), a clock time and a content hash. That is the schema the requirement asks for.

What it cannot do is differ: with no price source, the "opening" and the "closing" number are the same fair value, because nothing between the two captures changed it. The store is honest about that in the state column, and the price-data policy PDP-1 explains why inventing a move would be worse than publishing none.
