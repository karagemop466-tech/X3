What the official report actually contains

1027 rows across four weekly reads. The single most important number is the last column: most rows carry no game status at all. The report lists practice participation, and the practice participation is the bulk of the information. A game designation (Out / Doubtful / Questionable) appears only for players the club has actually ruled on.
