13 Week-11 markets, side then total then moneyline

The pipeline order is fixed

1. Side — a ridge fit on home margin gives each club an opponent-adjusted net rating; the fair spread is the negation of the expected margin, in betting notation. 2. Total — a second ridge fit on game total gives each club a pace rating. 3. Moneyline — derived from the side and the walk-forward residual sigma (currently 12.97 points), never fitted separately, so the three markets cannot contradict each other.

Each row carries its inputs: the chosen ridge penalty (margin 4.0, total 16.0), the number of training games, and the source. That is requirement R-15 — a probability with its inputs, not a pick.
