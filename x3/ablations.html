Ablations

With and without the feature, paired by game, with an interval

An ablation, not a penalty

The brief forbids subtracting a fixed number of points for a missing starter. So the same model is fitted twice — once with a verified availability feature and once without — and the difference is published with its uncertainty.

Result: delta MAE +0.461 (SE 0.461) on n = 2 test games. The interval straddles zero, so the data cannot separate the two models. That is a legitimate result and is published as one; it is not evidence for the feature, and it is not evidence against it.

n_test = 2 is far too small to settle anything. The design is what matters here: a paired comparison on a chronological split, with the coefficient fitted on the earlier week only.
