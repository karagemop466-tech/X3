What this desk is

It originates its own NFL numbers from verified data, grades them against a verified results feed, and ranks strategies on a proper score with signal and staking in separate columns. Where a number does not exist it is blank and flagged, never estimated.

Read the executive summary first, then decision readiness for the one row per requirement.

The four things that are actually measured

  • The side carries a signal, in-sample. 60 of 90 graded games went the model's way, Brier 0.2253 against 0.25 for a coin flip (Brier skill +0.099). It is in-sample: the model was written after these games, so gate G-1 fails and it cannot be promoted.
  • The total does not. 50 of 106 on direction, Brier skill -0.096 against a pre-registered constant benchmark of 44 points. Model MAE 11.91 against benchmark MAE 11.17.
  • The model is under-spread by a factor of 2.5. The predicted margin has SD 5.24 where the actual margin's SD is 12.91. Since the moneyline is derived from the margin and a residual sigma of 12.97, the probabilities it produces hug 0.5: a too-timid probability is unusable for staking even when its ordering is right.
  • Club depth order is an 80%-accurate guide to who plays. In the one official Game Book read, the club's first-listed player took the most snaps in 16 of 20 slots; 4 slots went to a backup instead. That is the measured basis for using official playing time rather than chart order.

What is blocked, and by what

Nothing can be staked, and no ROI or closing-line value can be computed, because no official market price exists in this repository — see the price-data policy PDP-1. The market column on every market row is blank on purpose. The readiness table gives the exact number that would clear each block.
