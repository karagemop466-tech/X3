Verification

How every row was checked, including the two bugs the checking found

Two bugs were found while verifying, and both inverted a published finding

  • The total metric was trivially true. The first version scored the total by asking whether the predicted total and the actual total had the same sign. Every total is positive, so the metric returned 106/106 — a perfect score from a broken test. Caught by reading the number, not the code: a 100% hit rate on NFL totals is not a finding, it is a bug. The metric was replaced with an error against a pre-registered constant benchmark.
  • A correlation joined on the wrong key. The snap-count correlation matched club and position strings that never coincided upstream, producing n = 0 — which was reported as an empty row rather than as a failure. Caught by asking why a correlation had no rows. The join now runs on the roster position family and reports its pairs.

Both are recorded because a verification log that only lists successes is not a verification log.
