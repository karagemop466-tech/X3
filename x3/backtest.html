Walk-forward predictions with the chosen penalty per row

The corpus, and the rule that makes it a backtest

138 finals are in the corpus and 106 are graded. A game is not graded until at least 30 earlier games exist, so the first two weeks train and never score. For every graded game the ridge penalty is chosen by an inner walk-forward inside the training window, so the penalty is never tuned on the week it is graded against. The chosen penalty is printed per row.

This is the pre-registered corpus for requirement R-19. Gate G-1 still fails, because the register was written after the games — the corpus is frozen now so that every game from here is out-of-sample.
