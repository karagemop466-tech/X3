Three kinds of venue evidence, never merged

Requirement R-23: the venue of every fixture, transcribed, with the kind of evidence on every row so the reader can weigh it.

3 of 27 rows come from a game centre's Game Info block or from a Game Book's own header — the strongest evidence. The other 24 come from the stadium named in the official week page's own ticket and hotel deep-links for that fixture, which is published by the league but is not a venue statement. The badge says which.

One row flags a naming problem rather than resolving it: the live week page's link still names FedExField for the Washington fixture while the club's own stadium is currently Washington's home under a different name. Neither name is corrected here — both are recorded (see the flagged page).
