Removal of luck

Four measurements that bound luck instead of pretending to delete it

Luck is bounded, never removed

The brief asks to "remove luck from the equation". It cannot be removed — a single NFL game is mostly noise. What can be done is to measure how much of a record is not skill, and then rank on a score that does not reward the noise. That is what this panel does.

Club by club: Pythagorean expected wins against actual wins

Pythagorean expectation from each club's own points for and against, compared with its actual wins. The league-wide sum is essentially zero (no systematic bias), but the club-level spread is SD 1.08 wins. That spread is exactly the luck a season-long win-rate leaderboard would reward, and it is why this desk ranks on Brier skill instead.
