Gates & demotion

Entry gates, integrity gates and the demotion rule that sends a strategy back to paper

The demotion gate, stated before anything is live

Requirement R-08 asks for a rule that sends a live strategy back to paper. D-1 has four triggers and any one fires it: a rolling 40-game hit rate at or below 0.50, a negative Brier skill over 40 graded games, four live weeks with no graded game, or a data-source schema failure. Nothing on this desk is live, so the gate cannot fire for real; the verification script therefore injects an adverse 40-game window and asserts that the rule fires. A rule that has never been executed is a wish, not a gate.
