Strategies & Validation Gates

Every strategy states a hypothesis, the signal that tests it, the official inputs it consumes, and the validation gates it must clear. A strategy that has not cleared the gates is shown for audit and is not eligible for reward.

Every strategy states a hypothesis, the signal that tests it, the official inputs it consumes, and the validation gates it must clear. A strategy that has not cleared the gates is shown for audit and is not eligible for reward.

Principle

All inputs are official NFL data. No commercial line, projection or injury feed is ingested. Every published line is originated by the platform's own line model.

Validation gates

A strategy must clear every gate to be eligible for scoring and reward. Thresholds are proposals pending the owner's decision on questions Q-008, Q-009 and Q-011; each is labelled accordingly.

GateNameRuleThresholdStatusWhy it exists
G1Data sufficiencyAt least the class minimum number of evaluated observations, spanning at least one complete season and at least two independent evaluation folds.Single 120 / Parlay 80 / Prop 100 / Prop combo 60 observationsproposedBelow these counts the confidence interval on the skill-share estimate is too wide for the result to mean anything.
G2Out-of-sample evaluationAll performance is measured by anchored walk-forward evaluation with purging and embargo, never by a random split and never on the training window.At least 3 evaluation foldsproposedRandom splits leak information across adjacent games and inflate measured edge.
G3False-discovery controlThe strategy's result must survive a Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate control applied across the register of every hypothesis tested in the cycle.FDR <= 0.10proposedA strategy found by trying many variants will look excellent in-sample even with no edge.
G4Temporal stabilityThe edge must be positive in a majority of evaluation folds and must have a consistent sign across seasons.Positive in >= 60% of folds; consistent sign across >= 2 seasonsproposedA single good block is not a skill; it is a sample.
G5Risk limitsPeak-to-trough drawdown and risk-adjusted return must sit inside the stated limits under the published stake policy.Max drawdown <= 20% of stake; Sharpe >= 0.50proposedA strategy that is skilled but unbounded in risk is not deployable.
G6Correlation and independenceThe strategy's own legs must not be strongly correlated, and the strategy must not duplicate exposure already held by another eligible strategy.Leg |rho| <= 0.35; |rho| vs eligible strategies <= 0.60proposedCorrelated bets multiplied into a parlay create fake edge and fake diversification.

G8Cost and margin adjustedThe edge must remain positive after the platform's stated margin and any commission or execution cost.Edge > margin + costsproposedAn edge that disappears inside the margin is not an edge.

Strategy register

ST-001 Scoring-efficiency spread

Hypothesis. A team's expected point margin is better predicted by its scoring efficiency relative to the league than by its win-loss record, so a model margin built from official points for and against, shrunk toward the league mean, will beat a naive record-based line once it is sufficiently different from it.

Signal. Expected margin = shrunk Pythagorean expectation of the league mean applied to the two teams' official points for and points against, plus a home-field term; signal = model margin minus the platform's own line.

  • Official weekly schedule and results — NFL.com weekly schedule

Note. Shrinkage weight for early-season teams is an open parameter (Q-024).

ST-002 Availability-adjusted game total

Hypothesis. The absence of a starting offensive player, replaced by a lower string, reduces expected scoring in a measurable way once the replacement's depth string is known, so a total adjusted for official absences should beat an unadjusted total.

Signal. Expected total from both teams' official scoring rates, adjusted by the depth string of each absent offensive starter as published in the club's official depth chart, then shrunk toward the league mean.

ST-003 Defence-discretion regression

Hypothesis. Defences that have performed far better than their underlying talent measure will regress towards it, so a team whose points allowed are well below its expectation should be faded, and vice versa.

Signal. Residual = actual points allowed minus the opponent-independent expectation from the team's official points for and against; signal = the regression adjustment applied to the spread.

PL-001 Correlation-budgeted two-leg parlay

Hypothesis. Most parlay edge in a naive construction is an artefact of multiplying correlated probabilities. A two-leg parlay restricted to legs whose signal correlation is below budget will show a materially lower but genuinely positive expected value, and the joint-priced probability will be higher than the product of the marginals.

Signal. Candidate legs are drawn from the eligible single-bet signals; any pair whose signal correlation exceeds the budget is excluded; the parlay probability is recomputed from the joint implied by the correlation structure rather than from the product of marginals.

PL-002 Half-season total pairing

Hypothesis. First-half and full-game totals from the same game are strongly related through pace, so a pairing across different games, restricted to low correlation, will price more honestly than a same-game pairing.

Signal. Two-leg parlay where both legs are totals from different games and the pair's signal correlation is within budget; the leg correlation is measured and reported, not assumed.

PP-001 Passer output against pass-defence concession

Hypothesis. A quarterback's passing output against a defence that concedes above its own expectation should exceed the quarterback's baseline rate, and this relationship holds out of sample once opportunity and opponent strength are controlled.

Signal. Baseline passing rate from official season passing statistics, adjusted by the opponent's official passing yards allowed relative to the league mean, adjusted by the quarter-back's official attempt share.

PP-002 Receiver volume share

Hypothesis. A receiver's share of his quarterback's attempts is more stable than his yardage, so a receptions line set from official attempt share will beat a yardage line once the target share is stable over a sufficient sample.

Signal. Target share from official receiving statistics, adjusted for the absence of higher-share receivers as listed in the official injury report, applied to the quarterback's official attempt projection.

Market
Receptions

prop

Blocked on per-game player logs (S-013).
Official inputs
  • Official player receiving statistics by season — NFL.com receiving statistics
  • Official weekly injury report — NFL.com official injury report

Note. The volume share is the more stable quantity; yardage is deliberately excluded from the signal.

PP-003 Workhorse rushing volume

Hypothesis. Backs with a high and stable attempt share will continue to receive a high attempt share, so an attempts line set from official rushing share will beat a yards line, which is contaminated by red-zone and game-script variance.

Signal. Attempt share from official rushing statistics, adjusted for offensive-line and depth-chart changes identified in the official injury report, applied to the team's official rushing-rate projection.

Rushing yards and attempts

Official inputs

  • Official weekly injury report — NFL.com official injury report

Note. Explicitly avoids the yards signal, which has higher variance for the same reason a prop line is hard to beat.

PC-001 Passer yards with team total

Hypothesis. Quarterback passing yards and team scoring in the same game are driven by the same game script, so the pair is positively correlated. Pricing the combination from the joint rather than the product of the marginals will produce a materially different, and more accurate, probability.

Signal. Two legs in one game, priced from a joint model conditioned on pace and scoring environment, with the conditional correlation measured on the official per-game data rather than assumed.

PC-002 Two-player volume pair across games

Hypothesis. Player volumes in different games are close to independent once each player's own opportunity is controlled, so a two-leg pair drawn from different games with no shared player has an effective number of independent legs of two, unlike a same-game pair.

Signal. Legs drawn from different games, with the same player prohibited from appearing twice and a leg cap applied; effective independent leg count is computed from the measured correlation matrix.

Explicitly out of scope

  • Any strategy whose signal depends on a commercial betting line, projection service or non-official injury feed.
    The brief requires the platform to originate its own lines and to verify every input against an official source.
  • Any strategy that infers a starter, a replacement or an availability state that no official source publishes.
    The brief forbids inferring values; unresolved depth is left blank and flagged.
