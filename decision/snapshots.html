Why this exists

The brief asks for the injury hub as "a timestamped series, not a weekly snapshot", for every read stored "with its clock time", and for the site to publish "the delta, never the level". Until this session the repository held one read of Week 10 and therefore no delta. Below are two hashed reads of the same official page, and what moved between them.

Captured through the authoring environment's page reader, because direct HTTP to nfl.com is blocked here (curl to www.nfl.com:443 fails with SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL). The hash therefore seals the transcription this store keeps, not the league's raw bytes; the store README states this rather than hiding it, and S-D3 is the suggestion that fixes it.

What this does not claim

Two points are not a series. The earlier read carries no clock time, so the gap cannot be stated in hours (F-D07). Two rows appearing cannot be attributed to the league rather than to a missed earlier transcription, because no sealed hash of the page as served exists for 2026-11-14 (F-D03). And the report still does not carry the reserve list, so an absent row means "not in this read", never "available".
