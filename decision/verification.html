Method

Line by line, from the artefact, with the link. Two disciplines are enforced mechanically rather than promised: no row on this tree may make a claim without a source cell that resolves - an official URL, or a repository path that exists and is checked at build time; and no number in the prose may be typed in, since every one is interpolated from a data file at build time and an unfilled key aborts the build.
