What "the residual" would look like

Three columns, published from the first day, two of them empty on purpose: fair line, market line, and the difference, each row carrying the timestamp of the read that produced it. An empty column is the mechanism that keeps a future number comparable rather than retrofitted - S-D12 in the queue. Until then, "our line versus the market" is arithmetically undefined here, and every page in this repository says so instead of estimating one.
