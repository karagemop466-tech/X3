What this tree is

Sixteen sessions each answered the same brief by building another site. This one adds no competing model, leaderboard or scraper. It is the review surface: a register of every requirement with the gate that blocks it, a queue of paths to choose between, the questions that must be answered first, and the one new dataset this session could legitimately create - a hashed, clock-stamped series of the official availability report, with the delta published instead of the level.

Statuses: PARTIAL 27 · READY 17 · BLOCKED 9 · NOT STARTED 8 · DEFECT 4 · DECIDED 2 · POLICY 2

Two findings a reader should not have to dig for

1. The report does not stop moving. Between a read on 2026-11-14 and a read at 2026-11-15T18:16Z on the same week, 13 game designations changed, 3 practice columns moved and 2 rows appeared, while one designation was withdrawn. They are concentrated on the two Monday-night clubs. So "the Week 10 report" is not one artefact, and a backtest cut weekly either leaks or starves depending on the game slot. See the series.

2. The pre-registered model applies fixed injury penalties, which the brief forbids. hub/data/prereg.json pins pos_cost QB 4.0 / RB 1.5 / WR 1.1 and a status weight, and hub/data/lines.json adds that as points to the margin on all 13 priced rows. The brief asks for an ablation with uncertainty instead. This is a decision, not a bug report - see F-D01 and S-D5.

And one unblock. Past seasons are reachable: the official page serves /injuries/league/2024/reg10 in full, and its own selector runs back to 1965. "Archive weekly reports for past seasons" moves from blocked to schedulable, and with it the train/validation/holdout split and the empirical calibration of "Questionable".

Start with the one decision that gates the rest

There is no market price anywhere in this repository - 0 of 13 priced games carry a market line - so ROI, closing-line value, rewards and the staking half of the leaderboard are all blocked on the same missing input, and three trees publish backtests anyway. The brief says the price-data policy must be decided before any backtest is published. Three options, with what each buys and costs.
