The position in one paragraph

The data layer is real and it is verified: 163 games with 137 finals reconciled to the official standings, 347 availability rows on 28 clubs re-read this session with hashes and clock times, 13 games originated with a fair spread and total, one order-checked three-market pipeline, and gates that rank on proper scores with confidence intervals instead of win rate. What is missing is not sloppy work but absent input: a single market price. There is no official NFL publication of prices, so the fair line exists for every market, the market line for none, and ROI, closing-line value, rewards and the staking half of the leaderboard are all one decision away from existing - or one decision away from being correctly declared out of scope. That decision (S-D1) is the most consequential line in this project.

What is genuinely new since the last session

A time series instead of a snapshot. Two sealed reads of the official report, one day apart, with the delta published: 13 designation changes, 3 practice-only moves, 2 rows appearing, and one designation withdrawn - almost all of it on the two Monday-night clubs. That settles an open design question: report "finality" belongs to a game slot, not to a week, so the capture cadence must be cut per kickoff (S-D6) or a weekly backtest leaks and starves at the same time.

The first settled originated line. Patriots 10, Lions 35: the side as originated (DET -3.5) covered, the total (46.5) landed under, and the margin error was 21.4 points against a predicted margin of 3.6 - the under-spreading flagged across the site, now with a dated, sourced example. One game, so it is published as a data point and nothing more.

An unblock. Past seasons are reachable on the official site (Week 10 2024 served in full; the selector runs to 1965), which converts archiving prior reports, a train/validation/holdout season split and the empirical calibration of "Questionable" from impossible to work (S-D4).

Three things a reader should be told, not shown a chart of

The model disagrees with the brief on injuries. The pre-registered model applies a fixed points cost per absent starter (QB 4.0, RB 1.5, WR 1.1, and a status weight of Out 1.0 / Doubtful 0.9 / Questionable 0.35) and adds it to the margin. The brief rejects exactly that and asks for an ablation with uncertainty (F-D01, Q-D11, S-D5). Both are defensible; only one is what was asked for, and the site should say which it is showing.

A wrong string is still published. The Munich venue appears on 3 served rows as "Alemun & Munchen", which no official page prints. A prior session corrected the correlation label in one tree and left the old one in another; the same fix-in-the-wrong-place pattern is why the venue survives (F-D02, L-07, S-D14). Both are minutes of work once someone owns one tree.

The raw club pages are on the public site. 32 depth captures and game-centre text sit under the publish root while the terms are unread (F-D04, S-D8). Moving the bodies out costs nothing analytically, because every derived row already links to the official page.

What this project should do next, in order

1. Decide the price-data policy (S-D1) and label every published backtest with it. 2. Choose one canonical tree and freeze the other 18 with a pointer (S-D2). 3. Put the capture on a per-game clock in CI with raw bytes and HTTP headers (S-D3, S-D6). 4. Backfill prior seasons and build the holdout (S-D4). 5. Replace the fixed injury penalty with an ablation and re-register (S-D5). 6. Add transactions/reserve and T-90 inactive captures (S-D7, S-D9), which is the only route from "a backup is suspected to start" to "a backup is confirmed to start".

Everything above is a choice this session deliberately did not make: the brief asks for a review section where paths are compared before work is implemented, so the register, the queue and the questions are the deliverable, and the queue is empty of implementations by design.
