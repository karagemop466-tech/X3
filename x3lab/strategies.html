How we remove luck

  • Shrinkage: raw win% is always published next to a beta(1,1)-shrunk estimate — a 5-2 record is not treated as proof of skill.
  • Binomial tests vs break-even: at -110 you need 52.38%; the Wilson 95% CI must clear it (gate G-6).
  • Deflated Sharpe: the more strategies tried, the higher the bar (gate G-7) — this is what makes the competition selectively skill-based.
  • CLV: beating the closing line is the cleanest skill evidence, and it is measured on every pick (gate G-5).
  • Correlation accounting: parlay/prop legs must publish measured correlation values or stay blocked (gate G-8).

Definitions and thresholds live in validation_gates.json. Pilot correlation values (n=16) are on the Leaderboard page and are explicitly not decision-grade.
