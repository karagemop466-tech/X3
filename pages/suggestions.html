Suggestions & Review Path

Improvement suggestions for the site and decision-making process are collected here. A reviewer chooses the best path before anything is implemented. Nothing is changed without review.

  How suggestions flow

  1. A suggestion is submitted (site improvement, data source, strategy, signal, or gate).
  2. It lands in the suggestions table below with status pending review.
  3. A reviewer assesses it against sources, validation gates, and the decision questions in section 3.
  4. The reviewer chooses: implement, defer, revise, or reject, with a reason.
  5. Only implemented suggestions are merged to the site; the rest stay flagged for review.
