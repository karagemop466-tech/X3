6Removing luck — skill-based design
  • Size positions by edge/confidence, not by fixed stakes.
  • Require correlation screening for all multi-leg bets (parlays, prop combos).
  • Backtest every strategy without look-ahead bias before it enters the leaderboard.
  • Decompose results into skill (repeatable edge) vs. luck (variance); reward skill only.
  • Gate every strategy on injury and backup-for-starter adjustments.
