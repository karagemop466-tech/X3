Executive Summary

Important details of the X3 NFL decision-intelligence project, outlined for leadership review.

Purpose

Collect, organize, analyze

Build a decision-support system for NFL betting lines: originate our own lines, generate useful signals, validate every strategy, track injuries and depth-chart deficiencies, and review suggestions before changes are implemented.

Core components

Six workstreams

(1) Suggestions & decision framework, (2) line-origination strategies & signals, (3) validation gates & correlation values, (4) single/parlay/player-prop combos, (5) injury & backup depth tracking, (6) leaderboard competition with rewards.

Data rule

Source or flag

Every value must be confirmed from an official source (e.g. NFL.com). Unconfirmed values are left blank and flagged unverified. Nothing is inferred.

Status

Live site

GitHub Pages site with landing page, category subpages, sourced tables, and a Flagged for Review page.
