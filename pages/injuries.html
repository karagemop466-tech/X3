3Deficiency rules
  • Flag a unit as deficient when the starter is out/questionable and a 2nd, 3rd, or 4th string backup is the next available player.
  • Track offense and defense separately; combine into a team-level availability score for line origination.
  • Update weekly with the official injury report and depth charts.
