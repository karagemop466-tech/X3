What this module is

Two brief items had no coverage anywhere in the repository: Q43 — archive weekly injury reports for past seasons, and Q44/Q45 — score injury-designation reliability by joining designations to the official inactive lists and measuring the play rate per designation with confidence intervals. This module does both, from official nfl.com sources only:

  • Archive — dated captures of the official Week-10 injury reports for 2023, 2024 and 2025 with sha256 seals (reliability/manifest.csv).
  • Reliability — play rate and inactive rate per designation with Wilson 95% intervals and n per row.
  • Player join — every designated player in the sample, the Game Book evidence for the outcome, and the unresolved rows.
  • Flagged for Review — render irregularities, ambiguous matches and the Out→played discrepancies. Nothing here blocks publication; nothing here is reconciled by guesswork.

What it means for the desk

  • Out is close to a reliable "will not play" statement in this sample, but not perfect: 4 Out-listed players have official playtime rows. Those rows are published as discrepancies, not reconciled — the report page exposes no last-updated timestamp, so designation-vs-kickoff timing is unknown (Q-04).
  • Questionable is a wide band: the sample play rate is high but the interval spans a range that cannot be collapsed to a single multiplier yet. Q45's calibration needs more weeks before any model use.
  • Doubtful behaves like a mild Out in this sample, with n too small to treat as more than a pilot number.

Scope decisions (disclosed before capture)

  • Week 10 of each season 2023–2025; the first four games as listed on the official report page (Thursday + early-Sunday window) — a convenience sample, not random. Three of the twelve games are international-series games (Frankfurt 2023, Munich 2024, Berlin 2025) because those kick off early.
  • 2026 Week 10 is not joined here: its report re-capture is claimed by another session (PR #79), and its Monday game was not final at capture time.
  • The archive stores report chunks 0–1 of each week (the sampled games' rows are complete within them), not the full 7–9 chunks — full-week archive is the next increment.

Suggestions (review → choose → implement — owner decides)

  1. Expand the archive to full weeks (chunks 2–9 of each report) so Q43 covers every game, not just the sampled window. First step: capture the remaining chunks for 2023–2025 reg10. Cost: storage only; no analysis blocked today.
  2. Join 2026 Week 10 once its re-capture (PR #79) lands, using the same pre-registered rules. First step: re-run this builder with one more season added to GAMES.
  3. Set the n threshold before any model use (R2-S6 already asks for this): the pilot intervals are wide; the owner should pick minimum n per designation before a Questionable multiplier is allowed anywhere near a signal.
  4. Bracket designation timing: capture the report page twice (Friday and Saturday) so Out→played rows can be separated into "designation held" vs "status changed after the page we read". First step: schedule two captures next sample week.
  5. Store full playtime sections for sampled games so a no-designation baseline can be computed (needed to show the designations actually carry information).

Questions, information, answers

QuestionWhat we know (this sample)What is still missing
What does Questionable mean? Play rate 54 / 79 = 68% (Wilson 95% CI 57–78%); inactive rate 17 / 56. Enough n to collapse the interval; other weeks/seasons; position-level splits.
Is Out reliable? Play rate 4 / 53 — and every played row is published as a flagged discrepancy with its Game Book link. Whether those were post-report status changes (report page has no last-updated timestamp, Q-04).
Is Doubtful different from Out? Pilot: 1 / 10 played — directionally similar to Out, n far too small to separate them. More Doubtful rows (they are rare); pooled multi-season n.
Can these rates feed a model yet? No. This module is descriptive and pre-registered as measurement only. Owner-approved n threshold (suggestion 3 above) and the baseline comparison (suggestion 5).
Is the archive complete? Report chunks 0–1 for three seasons, sealed with sha256; sampled-game Game Books stored. Full-week chunks; 2026 Week 10; any week other than Week 10.
Every table row on this module links to its official source or is explicitly flagged. Rates are descriptive measurements of the sample described on the Methods page — not a model input, not advice.
