Overview

Executive Summary

Purpose, current state, the decisions that are ready, the decisions that are blocked, and the recommended next actions.

Purpose

To give the owner a complete, verifiable basis for deciding how to act on NFL markets: a requirements register, an explicit list of what is known and unknown, a reviewable set of suggested improvements with alternative paths, a research programme for self-originated lines and strategies with machine-enforced validation gates, a competition that rewards skill rather than luck, and continuous official injury and depth tracking.

The five decisions that matter most

1 Is this a private analysis tool or a published line operation?

Everything downstream depends on it: the reward structure, whether a public leaderboard is appropriate, and whether a wagering interface may ever be built. It is question Q-001 and it is blocking.

2 Which market universe is in scope?

Spread, moneyline, total and which player-prop types. This determines the official data that must be ingested and, therefore, which of the three strategy classes can actually be competed. It is question Q-023 and it is blocking.

3 What are the gate thresholds that define eligibility?

Sample size, drawdown limit, skill-share threshold and correlation limit. Until these are set, the leaderboard can rank strategies but cannot reward them. It is question Q-008 and it is blocking.

4 May strategies be scored against an independent reference, or only against our own lines?

Judging a strategy against the model that priced the game measures agreement, not skill. Suggestion S-002 proposes a two-model separation that stays entirely inside the official-data rule. It is question Q-010 and it is blocking.

5 Is there budget for official per-game box scores?

Without per-game player logs the player-prop and prop-combination classes cannot be evaluated at all, so the competition would cover only two of the three classes the brief names. It is question Q-005.

What is deliberately not done

No commercial lines are ingested

The platform originates every line it publishes from official data. No betting feed, projection service or third-party injury source is used anywhere in the pipeline.

No value is inferred

Where an official source does not publish a value, the cell is left blank and flagged. Where a statistic cannot be computed because its dataset is missing, the statistic is omitted and the omission is recorded with its reason.

No change is applied without a decision

Suggestions are published with alternative paths and a recommendation, but every one of them stays in the proposed state until a reviewer chooses a path.

Flagged items do not block delivery

Unverified values and irregularities are published on the Flagged for Review page with the source link that was checked, so they can be reviewed after the site is live and acted on then. They do not block the platform, and they are never filled in by guesswork in the meantime.
