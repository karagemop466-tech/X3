Executive Summary

Key details, scope, status, and risks for the X3 NFL analytics project.

Executive Summary

X3 is a self-contained NFL analytics workspace that turns verified official data into decision-ready inputs for originating NFL betting lines and ranking skill-based strategies on a leaderboard. It is built to be reviewed, not trusted blindly: every value is traceable to an official source or explicitly flagged as unverified.

Objective

Collect, organize, and analyze NFL data using a full list of requirements; originate NFL lines; develop strategies that produce useful signals; treat strategy performance as a leaderboard competition with rewards for top performers; remove luck from the equation so that only selectively skill-based strategies are rewarded.

What is built (this release)

  • Verified league standings, Week 10 injury report, Week 10 schedule, and the Detroit Lions depth chart — all from official sources with per-row links.
  • A Requirements & Questions page listing the questions, information, and answers required before any decision.
  • A dedicated Suggestions page for review before implementation.
  • A Lines & Signals page with signal families, bet types, validation gates, and correlation methodology.
  • A Strategy Leaderboard framework with gates and a rewards structure.
  • An Injuries & Lineups page demonstrating starter/backup deficiency tracking with verified data.
  • Line-by-line Verification and a Flagged for Review page.

Key risks & open items

  • Lines are not NFL-published. No official source confirms betting lines; line values are left blank and flagged unverified.
  • Correlations and leaderboard performance need a data pipeline + backtest that does not yet exist; values are left blank and flagged unverified.
  • Decisions are pending. The Requirements page lists questions that must be answered before strategies are finalized and deployed.
  • Irregularities in source extraction (e.g., a contaminated injury block and an "unofficial" depth-chart label) are documented on the Flagged page.
