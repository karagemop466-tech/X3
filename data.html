Availability tracking requirements

  • Archive official report revisions with both publication and retrieval timestamps.
  • Represent status, practice participation, and game activation as separate fields; record the source's exact wording.
  • Link player IDs consistently across reports, stats, team and season. Preserve ambiguous identity as unresolved.
  • Measure defense and offense replacement effects from verified participation and snap counts; distinguish position group and scheme context. Do not equate injury designation with a quantified performance deficit.
  • Prevent leakage: use only the latest report available at the strategy's declared decision time.
Official league pages are starting points, not a claim that complete downloadable historical data or an API is available. Verify access, licensing, season coverage, and field definitions before use.
