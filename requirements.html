Requirements & Questions

The clear list of questions, information, and answers to address before any decision.

These are the questions, information, and answers that must be resolved before any decision can reasonably be made on the information we have and do not have. Each question notes the information needed and why it matters. Suggestions to discover missing information are on the Suggestions page.

Pre-decision questions

Question Why it matters / information needed Answer status Source
What is the exact definition of a "signal" and the required minimum sample size before a strategy is considered? Without a fixed threshold, strategies can be cherry-picked. A gate (e.g., minimum 1,000 historical bets or 3 seasons) must be set first. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
Which closing-line source will be treated as the skill benchmark (CLV)? Removing luck requires comparing bet outcomes to the market's closing line. The specific sportsbook/feed that publishes closing lines must be named and verified. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
What is the acceptable edge threshold to open a position (in half-points / EV)? Defines when a signal converts to a bet. Must be fixed before testing to avoid hindsight. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
What validation protocol is used (walk-forward, holdout, k-fold)? Determines whether a strategy's edge is real or overfit. Protocol must be chosen before any backtest. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
How are correlated parlays handled so risk is not understated? Correlated legs inflate variance; correlation values must be computed and legs capped/dropped when correlation exceeds a threshold. Unresolved — methodology designed, values pending data. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
Which injury/lineup sources are authoritative for starting-lineup changes (injury report vs. depth chart vs. game-day inactives)? The system must reconcile sources; conflicting data is a key risk. Source priority must be defined. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
How is "2nd/3rd/4th string" defined and where is it sourced per team? Depth charts are team-published and may lag; the authoritative source and refresh cadence must be fixed. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
What is the reward structure for the leaderboard (prize tiers, risk-adjusted metric)? The leaderboard metric (risk-adjusted ROI, CLV, significance) and reward tiers must be set before rankings are meaningful. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
What are the constraints on player-prop liquidity and book limits? Props have lower liquidity and book limits; strategy sizing must account for this or it is not implementable. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
What kill-switch / drawdown rule halts a strategy? A predefined stop-loss on drawdown prevents a luck-based strategy from running. Must be defined. Unresolved — needs decision. UNVERIFIED no official source; answer pending
