REQ-DATA-003Data & VerificationIf a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, leave it blank and flag it as unverified. Never infer or fill in values.
Rationale: Fabricated values are the single most damaging failure mode for a decision-support system.
Acceptance: Empty cells are rendered as an explicit 'unverified' marker, never as a zero, dash, or best guess; a Flagged for Review page lists every such cell.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14The validator counts blank cells per dataset and the Flagged for Review page reproduces them with their source link.
REQ-DATA-004Data & VerificationIngest official data programmatically and reproducibly rather than by hand.
Rationale: Hand-transcribed numbers are unauditable and drift.
Acceptance: scripts/fetch_official.py pulls only official domains, caches raw payloads, and records status, byte count, SHA-256 and UTC timestamp for each.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14data/datasets/fetch_manifest.json contains one record per source with sha256 and fetched_at.
REQ-DATA-005Data & VerificationWork only from official NFL and official club sources.
Rationale: Third-party aggregators introduce licensing and accuracy risk.
Acceptance: The source registry contains only nfl.com, operations.nfl.com and official club domains.		P0implementedhttps://www.nfl.com/scripts/lib/official.py is the single registry; no other host appears in it.
REQ-DATA-006Data & VerificationKeep the published data fresh without owner intervention.

Acceptance: A scheduled workflow re-fetches, rebuilds and republishes on a fixed cadence, and every table shows the age of the underlying fetch.		P1implementedhttps://www.nfl.com/injuries/The data-pipeline workflow runs on schedule; each table carries a 'data as of' timestamp.
REQ-STRAT-001Strategy ResearchOriginate our own NFL lines rather than consuming someone else's.
Rationale: The owner wants independent signal generation, not redistribution of a commercial book's numbers.
Acceptance: A published line model produces spread and total lines from official data, with a documented methodology and a stated margin; no commercial line is ingested.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14scripts/compute_signals.py emits data/datasets/our_lines.json; the Sources page states that no third-party line feeds are used.
REQ-STRAT-002Strategy ResearchResearch and develop strategies for generating useful signals.
Rationale: A signal is only useful if it survives validation and produces a measurable edge.
Acceptance: Every strategy has a stated hypothesis, an explicit signal definition computed from official data, a validation plan and a current status.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14strategies.html lists each strategy with hypothesis, signal, inputs and status.
REQ-STRAT-003Strategy ResearchTreat the work as a leaderboard competition that rewards top performing strategies.
Rationale: A competitive frame forces every strategy to be measurable and comparable.
Acceptance: A leaderboard specification defines entrants, scoring, gates, tie-breaks and a reward structure, and a live standings table is published.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14leaderboard.html publishes the scoring specification and the standings table generated from the backtest harness.
REQ-STRAT-004Strategy ResearchCover single bets, parlays and player-prop combinations.
Rationale: The three classes behave differently under correlation and variance.
Acceptance: The strategy register contains entries in each of the three classes and the leaderboard scores them separately.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14strategies.html groups entries by class and each class has at least one entry that reached the validation harness.
REQ-STRAT-005Strategy ResearchEvery strategy must pass validation gates before it can be deployed or scored.
Rationale: Without gates, the leaderboard rewards luck.
Acceptance: Ten gates (G1-G10) are defined, machine-evaluated from the backtest output, and a strategy that fails any gate is marked ineligible and excluded from rewards.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14The Gates table on strategies.html shows each gate, its threshold and the measured value for every strategy.
REQ-STRAT-006Strategy ResearchDetermine correlation values between signals and between strategies.
Rationale: Correlated bets multiplied in a parlay create false confidence and fake edge.
Acceptance: A correlation matrix is computed from official data and published with the method, sample and window stated.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14Correlations are computed by scripts/compute_signals.py and rendered on strategies.html with n and window.
REQ-STRAT-007Strategy ResearchRemove luck from the equation and build strategies that are selectively skill-based.
Rationale: A positive record can be pure variance; the platform must separate skill from luck explicitly.
Acceptance: A published skill-share metric decomposes observed outcome variance into model-implied variance and residual variance, and a strategy is only eligible when the skill share and its confidence interval clear a stated threshold.		P0implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14The luck/skill table on strategies.html shows skill share, residual share and the bootstrap interval for each strategy.
REQ-STRAT-008Strategy ResearchSelectively pursue strategies that are skill-based rather than betting broadly.
Rationale: Selective edge is the objective; volume is not.
Acceptance: The platform ranks strategies and surfaces only gate-passing entries; non-passing strategies remain visible for audit but carry no reward eligibility.		P1implementedProject brief, 2026-11-14leaderboard.html separates 'eligible' from 'ineligible' rows and explains the exclusion reason.
REQ-INJ-001Injury & Depth TrackingTrack injuries continuously from official sources.
Rationale: Availability is the largest single driver of NFL prices and of any honest edge.
Acceptance: The official weekly injury report is ingested, parsed and published per player with practice status, game status, injury designation and a source link.		P0implementedhttps://www.nfl.com/injuries/injuries.html publishes every parsed row; the count is reconciled against the official page.
REQ-INJ-002Injury & Depth TrackingKnow deficiencies in defences, including when second, third or fourth string backups are replacing starters because of the lineup.
Rationale: A defence missing its edge rusher is a different bet than one missing its third wideout.
Acceptance: For each team the platform tracks the depth string of the replacement for each absent starter, and publishes it with the official source for the depth ordering.		P0partialhttps://www.nfl.com/injuries/depth.html publishes the depth string for every official absence that can be resolved from an official depth chart. Where no official depth chart exists for the club, the cell is left blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page.
REQ-INJ-003Injury & Depth TrackingApply the same treatment to offences using starters or backups.
Rationale: Offensive depth is the mirror of defensive depth and moves offensive lines and totals.
Acceptance: Offensive absences are tracked with the same string-depth resolution and the same source requirement.		P0partialhttps://www.nfl.com/injuries/depth.html covers both units and flags clubs whose official depth chart could not be retrieved.
REQ-INJ-004Injury & Depth TrackingDistinguish a player who is absent from the report from a player who is healthy.
Rationale: Silence in an injury report is not a clearance.
Acceptance: Rows carry an explicit reported/not-reported flag and the site's methodology states that absence from the report is not evidence of availability.		P0implementedhttps://www.nfl.com/injuries/Methodology page states the rule and the data pages render the reported flag.
REQ-INJ-005Injury & Depth TrackingRecord the timestamp of every injury snapshot so historical analysis uses the report that was actually available at decision time.
Rationale: Back-testing with today's knowledge of an injury is look-ahead bias.
Acceptance: Each snapshot stores the official report's week, game date and the fetch timestamp.		P0implementedhttps://www.nfl.com/injuries/injuries.json records week, game_date and the manifest records fetched_at and sha256.
