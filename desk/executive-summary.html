Executive summary

What this desk is, what it has verified, what it can honestly claim, and the decisions that come next.

1. Status in one paragraph

The desk now ingests official NFL data end to end without manual input: 137 completed games, the full 32-club standings, four weeks of official injury reports and all 32 club depth charts. From those inputs it originates its own spreads and totals, publishes a strategy competition with an explicit gate ledger, and maps every Out or Doubtful player onto his club's depth string so start and backup absences are visible. The model is fitted and tested out of sample, and its honest performance is published next to it: a walk-forward correlation of +0.203 on 121 games with a mean absolute error of 10.51 points. That is a weak but real signal on a small sample — not a profitable system, and not presented as one.

2. What is verified, and how it was checked

  • Game results. Captured from the official weekly schedule pages. 137 finals across weeks 1-10.
  • Standings. W-L-T, points for and points against for all 32 clubs were recomputed from the captured game log and matched the official standings exactly. This is a genuine two-source reconciliation, because the two pages are separate publications.
  • Injuries. 1,372 rows across weeks 8-11, parsed with team headers and a fixed status vocabulary; anything outside that vocabulary would have failed the build.
  • Depth. 31 of 32 club charts are machine-readable. Houston publishes an image and is flagged rather than filled.
  • Deficiencies. 210 injury rows joined to a depth string — 142 at string 1, 68 at string 2 or deeper. 144 rows could not be joined and are listed with their reason.

3. What cannot be claimed, and why

  • No edge against the market. No official NFL source publishes spreads or totals. Every market cell on this site is blank and flagged. Without prices there is no closing line, no closing-line value, and no way to grade a strategy.
  • No validated strategy. All 10 strategies are hypotheses. The gate ledger shows exactly which gates each one fails. The leaderboard is empty by design.
  • No prop analysis. Player-prop history does not exist in any official source, so the prop-combo family is registered and blocked rather than approximated.
  • Luck is bounded, not removed. Walk-forward testing, a Pythagorean residual and confidence intervals bound it. A single NFL game is mostly noise, and no method changes that.

4. The five decisions that matter most

  1. Price source (S1). Choose a licensed feed or a declared manual record. Nothing scoring related can be finished until this is chosen.
  2. Sample size (S2). Add prior seasons so the ratings and the correlations mean more.
  3. Legal position (S9). Keep rewards notional until a legal opinion exists; no jurisdiction is asserted here without a source.
  4. Board structure (S4). Run an accuracy board now and a market-relative board once prices exist.
  5. Rule freeze (S12). Date-stamp the competition rules so later grading cannot be mistaken for hindsight.

5. How to read every number on this site

  • DERIVED computed from official values, with the method stated on the page where it appears.
  • UNVERIFIED could not be confirmed from an official source; left blank, never filled.
  • ok checked against an official page.
  • Plain numbers are transcribed from an official page and carry that page's link in the same row.

6. Integrity statement

The capture, parse, join, model and verification stages are all in the repository and all run in CI. The parser self-test fails the build if a parser returns nothing, so an empty dataset cannot be committed silently. The verification gate fails if a dataset row loses its source URL, if the game log and standings disagree, if a correlation sits outside its own confidence interval, or if a built page contains a broken link or an unfilled placeholder.

Evidence policy. Every value is either read from an official source, computed from official values and badged DERIVED, or left blank and marked UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
