Model caveats that must not be quoted without context

  • The model is fitted on 137 games from a single season. Ratings regress heavily and the early weeks were fitted on very few games.
  • Home-field advantage is measured as +0.08 points on this sample. That is smaller than the long-run convention, which is itself a finding worth review rather than a correction to be applied.
  • No injury adjustment is applied to the model. The deficiency page is diagnostic, not an input.
  • No neutral-site adjustment is applied. If a game is played outside a club's home stadium the home-field term is still applied unchanged.

Data irregularities observed during capture

  • NFL.com renders schedule scores client-side; the captured HTML shows "Hidden Score" in the score widgets. The visible score lines in the page text were used, and they reconcile exactly to the standings, which is the evidence they were read correctly.
  • An earlier session reported every club depth-chart URL as 404. On the capture date used here, 31 of 32 returned a parsable chart. Both observations are recorded; neither is deleted.
  • Injury-report row counts differ between sessions because they captured at different times. Only this build's single capture is used on this site.

Evidence policy. Every value is either read from an official source, computed from official values and badged DERIVED, or left blank and marked UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
