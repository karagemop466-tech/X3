Model and calibration

A deliberately small model: two ridge regressions on final scores. The point is not sophistication, it is that every parameter is measurable, reproducible and testable out of sample.

Walk-forward r+0.200n=121
MAE (points)10.52home margin
RMSE (points)13.82home margin
Bias-1.02positive = under-predicts home margin
Honest reading. A walk-forward correlation of +0.200 on 121 games is a weak signal. It is consistent with a real but small effect and is also consistent with noise at this sample size. It cannot support a profitability claim, and it is published with its uncertainty on the correlations page.
