Where each decision lives

Own lines →Week 10 originated spreads and totals with the measured gaps. Strategy competition →10 hypotheses, each with a gate ledger. Nothing is claimed to be profitable. Lineup deficiencies →102 starter-level absences and the next man up at each slot. Correlations →4 measured coefficients with n and 95% CIs. Open questions →18 questions, each with what we know, what is missing and the decision it blocks. Suggestions →12 proposals to review before any implementation.

Evidence policy. Every value is either read from an official source, computed from official values and badged DERIVED, or left blank and marked UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
