What this page can and cannot tell you. It tells you which clubs are reaching past their starters at a specific position. It cannot tell you how many points that costs, because that requires a model fitted on lineup outcomes — which needs several seasons of data. The strategy that tries to price it (S4/S5) is explicitly unvalidated.

Evidence policy. Every value is either read from an official source, computed from official values and badged DERIVED, or left blank and marked UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
