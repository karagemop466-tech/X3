Reward design (proposal, not active)

Rewards are notional and are listed as a proposal for review, not as a promise. Any real reward scheme needs three decisions recorded first on the Suggestions page: what is scored (model vs market), who can enter, and what happens when a strategy stops working. The legal status of staking real money is unresolved and is listed on the Flagged page — no jurisdiction is asserted without a source.

Evidence policy. Every value is either read from an official source, computed from official values and badged DERIVED, or left blank and marked UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
