Research protocol (proposed, not empirical results)

  • Maintain a row-level lineage: source URL, source publication time, retrieval time, event time, field definition, transformation, and version.
  • Define decision cutoff and compute features as-of that instant; immutable snapshots prevent future-report leakage.
  • Use chronological out-of-sample evaluation, compare calibrated forecasts with market-implied baselines, and include vig/fees and executable price.
  • Report calibration and proper scoring rules, net returns, uncertainty intervals, sample sizes, drawdowns, and all model trials. Wins alone do not prove skill.
  • For parlays, estimate dependence from joint out-of-sample data and evaluate actual offered payout. Never assume independent legs without validation.
  • Document all missing fields; leave unknown numbers empty rather than filling from memory or unofficial estimates.
The methods are recommendations, not findings sourced to the NFL. No current season injury, depth chart, odds, prediction, correlation coefficient, or strategy return has been verified in this repository.
