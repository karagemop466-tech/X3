Sources & Methodology

Every official source the platform reads, with its hash and fetch time, and the method behind every published number.

Source policy

Only official NFL sources (nfl.com, operations.nfl.com) and official NFL club sites are used. No commercial betting line, projection service, injury feed or third-party aggregator is ingested anywhere in the pipeline. Every payload is recorded with its SHA-256 and UTC fetch time so any published number can be traced to the exact bytes it came from.
