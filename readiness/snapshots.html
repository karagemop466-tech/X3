Snapshot capture

Scheduled, hashed, immutable availability snapshots - the design, its status, and the phases blocked on official sources.

Requirement: immutable multi-day availability snapshots captured on a schedule with retained hashes, so practice designations can be replayed as they were known. The workflow .github/workflows/availability-snapshots.yml is committed and runs on the schedule above once merged to the default branch. Until its first scheduled runs execute, the repository holds one static capture plus the stored weeks 8-10 report reads - published honestly as pending, not claimed as running.
