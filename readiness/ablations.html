Injury / personnel ablation

The same model with and without the availability features, chronological holdouts, the difference reported with uncertainty - replacing fixed injury penalties.

Same model form (home constant + 32 club margin effects, ridge lambda 2.0), identical training windows. Arm A = published ratings only; Arm B = identical ratings plus the two official availability-load features (0-4 scale, derived from the official injury report joined to club depth charts). MAE delta > 0 means the availability arm is closer to the truth. Paired bootstrap, 10000 resamples, seed 20261115.
