Why the probe log is the deliverable before publication

The report page carries no last-updated timestamp (standing question Q-04), so the only way to bracket designation timing is to store dated reads. Before the league publishes, the honest capture is the probe itself: it proves the endpoint state at a recorded clock time. Once a probe observes a Week-11 report, the capture (raw evidence + sha256 in data/week11/captures/) and the per-club designation table are added here and never back-filled.
