Why a freshness audit instead of a new dataset

Three prior sessions already publish canonical registers for these datasets (atlas and closeout), each guarded by its own CI gate. Re-publishing them would create a fourth competing copy; instead this module re-reads the official pages and diffs them field by field against what is committed, so drift would fail loudly here and in scripts/week11_verify.py - the same fail-loudly rule the drift-alarm requirement (B62) pins on every scraper.
