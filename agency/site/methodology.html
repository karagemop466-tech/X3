Methodology

How the numbers are produced, line by line.

  • Margin model: ridge regression on 138 finals (weeks 1–10), design = intercept (home advantage) + 32 club ratings; lambda chosen by nested walk-forward over {1,3,10,30,100}.
  • Pythagorean alternative: club rate sqrt(PF/(PF+PA)) fitted to margin by least squares.
  • Total model: ridge total = c + off_home + def_away + off_away + def_home, lambda 10, refit weekly. Published beside a naive club-mean control until one wins.
  • Probability: P(home wins) = Phi(margin / sigma), sigma = 11.34 from walk-forward residuals.
  • Brier skill vs constant 0.5, game-cluster bootstrap CI, Benjamini-Hochberg q = 0.10 multiplicity adjustment.
  • Luck residual = actual win% − Phi(mean margin / sigma).
  • Correlations: Pearson r with n and Wilson 95% CI, official sources only.
  • Injury signal: transitions between consecutive official weekly reports; no level is ever a feature.
Source discipline: official NFL pages (nfl.com Game Books, standings, schedules, injury report and club depth charts) only. Values that cannot be confirmed officially are left blank and flagged unverified. Nothing here is inferred. Outputs are deterministic: a fresh engine + site build reproduces the committed files byte for byte (CI reproducibility gate).
