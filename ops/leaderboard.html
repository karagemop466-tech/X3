Leaderboard - signal and staking, kept apart

The leaderboard has two columns that are not allowed to talk to each other. Signal asks "did the call beat a coin flip, and by how much, with what n and what interval". Staking asks "what would that have returned at a real price". A strategy is promoted on the signal column alone. That is the whole point: a lucky staking run - a hot streak at generous prices - cannot promote a strategy whose calls are no better than a coin flip, because staking is not read during promotion.

Why every strategy is still in paper

The blocking gates are listed per row. In aggregate: no strategy has reached the 200 graded bets G-2 requires (88 is the largest sample), the multiple-testing control has not been run because it needs that sample (G-3), and no market price exists anywhere in the repository (G-5), which alone blocks staking, ROI and CLV. A strategy in paper is not a failure of the analysis; it is the analysis working.

Rewards

No reward is configured, and none is promised. Rewards for a top-performing strategy require a decision this desk cannot make alone: what is scored (signal, staking, or both), who pays, and under what rules. A reward paid on staking alone would reward luck by construction, which is exactly what this page exists to prevent. Options are on Suggestions, S-05.

How a strategy enters the competition

  1. Write the rule, the inputs and the staking plan into the repo before the first graded bet (G-0).
  2. Grade walk-forward only, weeks before the graded week (G-1).
  3. Reach 200 graded bets (G-2), then run the FDR control across every tested strategy (G-3).
  4. Attach a verified market price and beat it out of sample (G-5, G-6).
  5. Stay live until a demotion gate fires (Gates &amp; demotion).
