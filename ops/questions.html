The questions that actually gate the project

  1. Can this repository legally hold market prices? A licensed feed changes everything downstream; recording public board prices may require permission. Until answered, the market column stays blank.
  2. What is being scored and rewarded? Signal, staking, or both. A reward paid on staking alone rewards luck, which the split on the leaderboard exists to prevent.
  3. Is a club depth chart an acceptable input? Most say "unofficial" and few print an as-of date.
  4. How many seasons of history are available? The 200-bet skill gate cannot be met on one partial season at any plausible bet volume.
