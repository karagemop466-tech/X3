Replacement depth - who replaced whom, from which string

The requirement is not "did somebody replace the starter" but "who, from which string, and does the club even have a body there". This page joins the official injury report to each club's own depth chart and publishes two separate facts about every absentee: which column the club prints him in at his position (1 = the starter's column) and which tier of that column he is (1 = the top name). The man below him is named only when he is a real player at the same position code - never a tight end standing in for a receiver because they happen to share a section of the page.

Why there is no single "depth string" number. The first draft of this page printed one number and it was wrong in two directions: a club that prints three WR columns made its third starter look like "depth 5", and a group-wide ladder made a tight end look like the replacement for a receiver. Both are fixed by publishing the column and the tier separately and refusing cross-position replacements. Where a club lists a player twice (NE Ben Brown ,TEN Tommy Townsend), the duplicate is reported rather than silently de-duplicated.

Coverage of the source

1 of 32 captured charts print an as-of date, and 14 of 32 are labelled "unofficial" by the club that publishes them. Both facts are why a depth-sourced adjustment is badged and never fed into a price (F-05).
