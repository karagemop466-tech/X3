Verified results feed

A verified results feed is the floor under everything else: without final scores there is no backtest, and without a backtest there is no ROI. This feed is rebuilt from official nfl.com week pages, then reconciled against the official league standings - two separately published official pages that must agree. They do, on all 32 clubs and all 137 finals.

Why this is a real check and not a formality. The game log and the standings are different pages produced at different times. If a score were mistyped, or a game missed, the club's points-for would disagree with the standings page. All 32 clubs reconcile across 137 games, so a transcription error in either source would have to be replicated identically in the other to slip through.

What the feed does not carry

  • No closing line. A result is a score, not a price; the price that settled a bet is not in this repository (F-01).
  • No player-level box score. The prop backtest runs on the official per-week receiving logs on each player's own page (props), not on a full box-score feed, so it covers the players we captured and nobody else.
  • No historical seasons. One partial season only, which is why the sample gate cannot be met.
