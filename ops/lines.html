Originated lines - fair, market, difference

Our own numbers for the week-11 slate, in the order they are originated: side, then total, then moneyline. The fair line is what our model says the game is worth. The market line is what a sportsbook would charge, and it is blank because this repository contains no official market price - inventing one to fill the column would be the single most damaging thing this page could do. The difference column stays blank for the same reason and is computed automatically the moment a feed is attached.

How to read the side column. Negative = the home club is favoured, in the same notation a sportsbook uses. The number is derived from a ridge-shrunk net rating fitted only on weeks before the game, so it never sees a result it is being graded on. Parameters are printed on the method page.
