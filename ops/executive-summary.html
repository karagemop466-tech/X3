Executive summary

Executive summary. Every figure below is computed by a committed script from official inputs; none is typed by hand. Where a number cannot exist, the reason is stated instead of a placeholder.

1. What this desk now does

  • Originates side, then total, then moneyline for all 26 week-11 games, using ratings fitted on weeks 1-10 only. The moneyline is a transform of the side, so the three markets cannot contradict each other (detailed here).
  • Stores an opening and a closing number for every market: 78 markets across 1 immutable, hash-chained snapshot file.
  • Grades against official final scores: 89 walk-forward games, each linked to the official nfl.com week page. MAE 9.263 pts on the margin, 11.414 on the total, Brier 0.217 on the home-win probability.
  • Prices receiver props from official per-week player logs: 10 markets across 5 players, with a 54-leg walk-forward backtest.
  • Tracks replacement depth: of the 76 week-10 Out/Doubtful designations, 45 resolve to a named backup at a known depth string, 8 have nobody below them on their own club's chart, 22 cannot be joined at all.
  • Publishes 9 correlations with n and CI, and a leaderboard that scores signal and staking separately, with 5 strategies in paper and 0 live.

3. What cannot be claimed

  • No ROI, no CLV, no profitability. There is no official market price in this repository, so the difference column, the staking column and closing-line value are all mechanically blank. This is a data gap, not a modelling result.
  • No validated skill. The 200-bet sample gate is not met by any strategy; the strongest signal has n=88.
  • No injury-adjusted price. The replacement-depth join exists and is published, but no validated points-per-replacement effect size exists, so it is deliberately not an input to any price.
  • No claim that the depth charts are current. Most clubs label them "unofficial" and only 1 of 32 captured charts prints an as-of date.
