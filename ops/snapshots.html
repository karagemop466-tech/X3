The chain

Each snapshot stores the SHA-256 of its own content plus the hash of the snapshot before it, so a retroactive edit to any stored number breaks the chain at that point and every point after it. Verify with python3 scripts/ops_verify.py, which recomputes every hash.

What this store cannot do yet. It cannot measure closing-line value, because the closing market line is the thing that does not exist. It also cannot show a true opening price: the first capture is by definition the first time we looked. Both limits are published as F-01 and F-02.
Built by scripts/ops_build.py from data/ops/*.json. Every table row links to an official source or is badged. No market price exists in this repository, so market columns are blank by policy, never estimated. Shared task board: COORDINATION.md.
