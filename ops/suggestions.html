Suggestions for review

A review queue, not a changelog. Nothing on this page has been implemented; each row is a proposal with its rationale, its cost, its risk and the decision it needs. Read the list, pick a path, and the chosen row moves to "adopted" with a note saying who chose it and when.

How to choose. The rows are ordered by leverage per unit of effort. The first two unlock entire columns of the desk that are currently blank. The middle rows harden what already exists. The last rows are cheap polish. Nothing here changes a published number without re-running the verification gate.

Effort and impact are engineering judgements, clearly labelled as such - they are not data.

Suggested order, if you only do three

  1. Attach a price source. It is the single change that turns four blank columns into real ones.
  2. Widen the history. Two or three prior seasons turn a 88-game backtest into something that can meet the 200-bet gate.
  3. Decide the reward rule. Otherwise the competition has no finish line.
