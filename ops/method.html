Procedure, exactly

  1. Read the official week pages, keep one row per game with both scores, the week, the date and any neutral-site banner.
  2. For a game in week W: fit the side on finals from weeks < W only; fit the total the same way.
  3. Side = ridge regression of final margin on club identity plus fitted home field; neutral sites carry no home-field term. Total = ridge regression of game total on club pace plus a base.
  4. Moneyline = Phi(side / sigma), so the probability is a function of the spread and cannot contradict it.
  5. Grade against reality on the following week. Never fit on the game being graded.
  6. Attach a market price, if one exists. None does, so every market column stays null with reason NO_OFFICIAL_SOURCE.

Statistical machinery

  • Pearson r with a Fisher z 95% interval and a two-sided p from Student t computed through the incomplete beta function - no table lookup, no approximation by eye.
  • Wilson score interval for every proportion (hit rates, over rates). It behaves correctly at small n and near 0 or 1, which is where an NFL season lives.
  • Ridge least squares solved by Gaussian elimination on the normal equations; the matrix is at most 33x33 and positive definite.
  • SHA-256 hash chain over every snapshot, so a stored number cannot be edited after the fact.
