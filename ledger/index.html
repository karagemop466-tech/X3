What this ledger can and cannot support yet

  • Results: supported. 32 of 32 clubs match the official standings on W, L, T, points for, points against, home and road records. Source: nfl.com standings.
  • Prices: not supported. No official source publishes 2026 opening or closing lines, so every opening and closing cell is blank. ROI and closing-line value cannot be computed until a price source is chosen.
  • Signal vs staking: specified, not measured. No strategy has settled bets, so the leaderboard holds no rows.
  • Demotion gate: proposed. Thresholds are proposals for an owner decision, not adopted rules.
Every table row links its official source or is marked UNVERIFIED. DERIVED values are calculations from official inputs, not predictions. This site places no bets and pays no rewards. Built by tools/build_ledger.py; checked by tools/verify_ledger.py.
