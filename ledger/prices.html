Lines & price store

Model lines are our own (DERIVED). Market columns are blank: no official NFL source publishes 2026 point spreads, totals or moneylines, so nothing is filled in. The difference column is computed only when both fair and market values exist; with no market values it is blank.

IRREGULAR Three model rows (BUF@NYJ, CAR@NO, JAX@TEN) list the home and away teams the opposite way to the official schedule. Their fair spread is therefore blank. The fair total is kept, since a total does not depend on which side is home. Source: nfl.com week 10 page (e.g. "Bills at Jets", "Panthers at Saints", "Jaguars at Titans").
