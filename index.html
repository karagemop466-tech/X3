What needs a decision from you

The questions below block a decision that cannot reasonably be made on the information currently held. Each one names what is known, what is missing and how to get it.

Q-001 Is this platform for private analysis of games the owner bets on, or is it intended to publish lines that other people bet against (i.e. operate a book)?

The two answers produce completely different platforms. Publishing a line with a stake attached is a regulated activity in most jurisdictions; analysing one's own bets is not. Every downstream design decision - prize structure, 'rewards', public leaderboard - depends on this.

Blocks: Reward design, whether the leaderboard is public or private, whether a wagering interface may ever be added, and the entire compliance workstream.

What we know
  • The NFL publishes its official playing rules and rule changes through NFL Football Operations. (NFL Football Operations - 2026 NFL Rulebook)
  • The NFL publishes official league statements, including policy positions, through NFL Communications. (NFL Communications (NFL.com))
What is missing
  • Whether the platform will accept, hold or publish stakes.
  • Whether the leaderboard rewards real money, a points-based allowance, or recognition only.
  • Whether lines are made available to any third party.
How to obtain it
  • Answer the question in one sentence to the platform owner.
  • If the answer is 'publish lines to others', commission a jurisdiction-specific gaming-licensing review before any line is published.
  • If the answer is 'private analysis', record that decision in this register so the leaderboard reward design can be settled.
Q-002 Which states or countries will any wagering activity be conducted from or into?

Sports-betting legality, licensing and tax treatment are jurisdiction-specific; a single global design can be illegal in a subset of target markets.

Blocks: Whether any transactional feature may be built at all; the compliance gate in the release checklist.

What we know
  • Nothing in this area has been confirmed from an official source yet.
What is missing
  • Legal entity and location of the operator.
  • Target jurisdictions and whether they are permitted for the intended activity.
  • Whether geofencing, identity checks and responsible-gambling controls are required.
How to obtain it
  • Obtain a written legal opinion or a licensed compliance review for each target jurisdiction.
  • Record the permitted activity type per jurisdiction in a compliance matrix before development continues.
Q-003 Do NFL rules or club policies restrict personnel with access to this platform from betting on games?

Personnel with access to non-public injury and line information can be subject to league restrictions that would materially change who may operate the system.

Blocks: Who may be granted access to injury data and to unpublished lines.

What we know
  • The NFL's official rulebook and rule-change summaries are published by NFL Football Operations. (NFL Football Operations - 2026 NFL Rulebook)
What is missing
  • The specific personnel policy section that applies to staff with access to the platform.
  • Whether the platform's operators are in scope.
How to obtain it
  • Read the current rulebook section on gambling and personnel for the 2026 season.
  • Have counsel confirm applicability to the operating individuals and entities.
Q-004 What is the required history depth for back-testing (how many prior seasons of official data must be retained)?

Sample size drives every confidence interval on the site. Three seasons cannot distinguish skill from variance in NFL betting; one season cannot support a parlay strategy at all.

Blocks: Validation gate G1 thresholds and the first leaderboard eligibility date.

What we know
  • Official standings, schedules and player statistics are published by NFL.com for prior seasons, and the pipeline already ingests 2023-2026. (NFL.com league standings (season/year selectable))
What is missing
  • Minimum seasons required before a strategy may be scored as eligible.
  • Whether the owner will fund historical data that NFL.com does not publish at game level.
How to obtain it
  • Decide the minimum sample size per strategy class and encode it as gate G1.
  • Extend the season list in scripts/lib/official.py if more history is needed - the fetcher already handles arbitrary seasons.

How to read this platform

Verified means the value was read from an official NFL or official NFL club page, and the row links to it. Unverified means the official source does not publish that value, so it is left blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page. No value on this site is inferred, estimated or filled in from a proxy. Where a statistic could not be computed because the required official dataset is missing, the statistic is omitted and the omission is listed with the reason.
