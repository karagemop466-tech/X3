The official NFL links document the league data/source class; repository links show the project’s derived work and policy. A link is not evidence that a blocker is cleared. Unknown values remain blank and flagged. The full requirement/question registers and reviewable choices are in Requirements, Questions & information gaps and Suggestions.

Trading desk - the eight operating requirements

Registration, not replacement. ops/ is a separate trading-desk tree that answers the eight operating requirements directly and says plainly where it cannot: prices originated in a fixed order (side, then total, then moneyline), a price-snapshot store with opening and closing schema for every market, correlations that each carry their n, injured starters resolved to the depth of their replacement, and a signal-vs-staking leaderboard whose demotion gate is dry-run to prove it fires.
