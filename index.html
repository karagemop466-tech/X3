02 / FORM HYPOTHESES

Signal library

Candidate features only. These are research proposals, not demonstrated predictive edges.

NO PERFORMANCE CLAIMS
01

Availability-adjusted unit strength

Estimate impact by position, starter status and replacement tier; compare forecast with and without confirmed absences. Keep report status, source, and timestamp separate.

Needs: official injury reports · active/inactive records · depth chart · player participation
PROPOSED
02

Matchup efficiency, not raw rank

Test play-level offensive efficiency against defensive context (down, distance, field position, opponent and game state); shrink small samples toward a baseline.

Needs: play-by-play · stable definitions · season / opponent adjustment
PROPOSED
03

Market movement & price quality

Record time-stamped prices and compare a model's fair probability to the executable price net of vig. Evaluate closing-price movement without treating it as guaranteed profit.

Needs: licensed/authorized historical odds snapshots · limits · closing timestamp
PROPOSED
04

Role-sensitive player props

Model opportunity (routes, carries, targets, snaps) separately from conversion; condition on opponent, usage and verified availability, not only recent box scores.

Needs: official stats / play-by-play · participation · market history
PROPOSED
03 / EARN THE RIGHT TO COMPETE

Validation gates

Pre-register each strategy, freeze its rules, and preserve the audit trail. Failure at any gate means no leaderboard reward.

GATES REQUIRED
01

Data & timestamp audit

Document source, coverage, revisions and point-in-time availability. Exclude information published after bet placement.

PASS: reproducible dataset manifest
02

Pre-registration

Lock hypothesis, feature set, markets, staking, thresholds, selection rules and evaluation metrics before holdout.

PASS: immutable version/hash
03

Walk-forward testing

Chronological train/validation/test; tune only on past data. Use season and time-based splits, not random game shuffles.

PASS: untouched future holdout
04

Leakage & multiplicity

Audit look-ahead, target leakage, repeated trials and correlated variants. Track every experiment; adjust inference for multiple testing.

PASS: full experiment registry
05

Uncertainty & robustness

Report sample size, confidence intervals, drawdowns and calibration. Stress test seasons, price assumptions and plausible costs.

PASS: edge survives stress tests
06

Live shadow period

Log timestamped picks at executable prices before any award. Compare live calibration, ROI and closing-line value with preregistered expectations.

PASS: minimum exposure set in advance
Leaderboard principleReward repeatable, risk-adjusted, net-of-cost performance on audited out-of-sample picks—not win rate, gross ROI, or a lucky short streak.Metrics to publish together: net ROI / yield · log loss or Brier score (probability quality) · calibration · closing-line value · max drawdown · bet count · uncertainty interval.
04 / COMBOS ARE NOT INDEPENDENT

Correlation & bet construction

Parlay pricing must use the joint outcome distribution. Multiplying leg probabilities assumes independence and is unsafe for related outcomes.

BET TYPERESEARCH METHODREQUIRED CHECKSSTATUS
Single betsEstimate fair probability per outcome; compare to available price after vig/costs.Calibration by market; proper scoring; net return; uncertainty; line movement.Framework only
ParlaysEstimate joint probability (conditional model / simulation) and validate joint calibration.Same-game dependence, payout terms, correlation restrictions, price, tail risk.Framework only
Player prop combosModel shared game script, snaps/opportunity and player-level dependencies jointly.Role and inactive changes; teammate/opponent effects; joint holdout evaluation.Framework only
How to determine correlation: define the paired outcomes and conditioning set first. On a strictly held-out sample, report Pearson correlation for continuous outcomes and/or tetrachoric/phi association for binary outcomes, with sample size and uncertainty intervals. For betting combinations, also test conditional joint probabilities and calibration. Correlation is population- and context-dependent; no universal value is supplied here.
