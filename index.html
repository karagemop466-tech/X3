Executive summary for the decisions, Originated markets for the three-market board, Price store for the open/close record, Results feed for the reconciled scores, Replacements for who is actually starting, Suggestions to choose a path, Open questions for the information gaps, and Flagged for review for everything that could not be verified. The decision council restates the eight requirements of the brief as pass/fail acceptance tests, and holds the suggestion queue where a path is chosen before anything is implemented. signals/ originates its own side, total and moneyline in that order, stores an opening and a closing number for every market, and ranks strategies on signal with staking reported separately. x3/ is the 2026 desk: 28 pages built from official NFL sources only, gated by a check script that fails the build on a broken link, an unsourced row, or a fabricated figure.

Earlier sessions published 12 overlapping site trees; they are preserved and indexed at hub/archive.html. hub/ is the maintained tree. The repository is karagemop466-tech/X3 and the shared task board is COORDINATION.md.
