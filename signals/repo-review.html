Irregularities found in the review

  • 16 trees, one subject. A reader asking "what is our number for this game" can find 16 different answers. Only hub/ is presented as canonical.
  • The same injury report was transcribed twice into two schemas. Both agree on 345 of 345 game statuses, which is the strongest evidence in the repository that the injury layer is right. The disagreement on one practice status is logged rather than adjudicated.
  • A previously published correlation was wrong. An earlier session published r(game total, |margin|) = −0.161; recomputing from the same 150 finals gives −0.016, and −0.161 is in fact r(total, signed home margin). The pipeline was fine; the label was wrong. This module recomputes both and prints them separately.
  • Depth charts are the weakest link. No as-of date, and most are labelled unofficial by the club that publishes them. They are used as a report, never as a model input.

Every item above is already published on the Flagged for review page. None of it blocks the site; all of it is visible.
