Verification log

Line by line, and enforced by a script rather than by assertion. scripts/verify_signals.py runs after every build and again in CI; it fails the build on any broken internal link or any table row that neither links an official source nor carries an unverified marker.

What the verifier does not check

The verifier proves provenance and structure, not truth. It confirms that a number has a source and that the row is not silently invented. It cannot confirm that a transcribed page was transcribed correctly — that needs a human to open the link, which is why every row carries one.

How each layer was verified

Results
Read from data/desk/games.json, which was captured by a GitHub-hosted CI runner straight from the official week schedule pages. Each row keeps the URL of the page it came from.
Standings and luck
Read from data/desk/standings.json and data/desk/luck.json, same capture.
Injuries and depth
Read from data/desk/injuries.json, data/desk/deficiencies.json and data/desk/depth.json. Absence rows carry both the league injury page and the club's own depth chart, plus the club's unofficial label where it has one.
Lines, snapshots, scores
Computed here by scripts/x3_signals.py from the rows above. Every row inherits the source URL of the games it was fitted on.
Market prices, ROI, props
Nothing to verify: no such row exists. Empty and flagged.
