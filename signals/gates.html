12 gates. A strategy must clear every blocking gate before it can be promoted; nothing in this module has.

The demotion gate — live to paper

A strategy that is live is sent back to paper the moment any of these fires. The point of writing them down before anything is live is that the rule cannot be renegotiated under pressure after a bad month.
