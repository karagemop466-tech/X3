What is deliberately NOT a source

  • Sportsbooks. The NFL has official sportsbook partners, but they are commercial operations, not open official feeds, and no price from them is used anywhere on this site.
  • Third-party statistics sites. Only league and club pages are used, so that every number can be traced to the body that produced it.
  • Anything inferred. A value that would have to be guessed is left empty and flagged.
