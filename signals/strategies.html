Strategy register

15 strategies in three families: singles, parlays and player-prop combos. Every one is pre-registered before its numbers are computed, and every one is blocked. The register exists so that the trials are on the record — that is what stops a future good run from being mistaken for a discovery.

G4, multiple-testing control. Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.1 was applied to the 6 strategies with a computable p-value. 3 are discoveries. Two of them point the wrong way: the selective total is reliably worse than a constant. A discovery with an adverse direction is a finding, not a skill claim, and it is labelled as such rather than buried.

Why the prop family is empty

UNVERIFIED (F-04) A player-prop market needs official per-game player statistics. This repository holds none: the capture covers week schedule pages, standings, injury reports and club depth charts. All four prop-combo strategies (R1–R4) are registered with their rules written down and every value empty, so the moment the data lands they can be scored without anyone re-litigating the design. See suggestion S-02.

Why nothing is promoted

Two gates block everything, and neither is a matter of opinion:

  • G2, sample size. 105 graded games against a pre-registered threshold of 200.
  • G5/G6, benchmark and uncertainty. These can only be evaluated against a market price for ROI. Without one, the strongest statement available is "closer to the truth than a constant", which is what every score on this page measures.
