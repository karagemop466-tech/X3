The one that matters most

F-01, no official market price. The NFL does not publish betting lines or odds. Its official sportsbook partners and its official data distributor are commercial feeds, not open ones. Until that changes, ROI, closing-line value, staking, drawdown and the prop markets are not approximately known — they are unknown, and this site says so on every page where they would otherwise appear.
