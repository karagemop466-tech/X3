What this module does

It originates its own NFL numbers — side, then total, then moneyline — from verified league data, stores an opening and a closing number for every market it originates, grades them walk-forward against what actually happened, and scores strategies on signal separately from staking. It does all of that without a single market price, because none exists in this repository. That is not a gap in the analysis; it is the finding that shapes everything on this site.

The three numbers that matter

Side beats a constant baseline
0.644 of 118 graded games (95% CI 0.554–0.725, exact binomial p = 0.0022). This clears the multiple-testing gate. It is real, and it is the only favourable discovery we have.
Total does not
0.441 of 118 (p = 0.2313). Worse: when the total model is most confident it is reliably wrong — 0.312 on 32 selective games, p = 0.0501. That is a significant adverse finding, not a null.
The model is under-spread
predicted margin SD 4.810 against an actual SD of 13.166 (ratio 0.365). It ranks teams in roughly the right order but pulls every number toward the league mean.
