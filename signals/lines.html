Originated lines

Three markets, originated in a fixed order and printed with three columns each: our fair line, the market line, and the difference. The market and difference columns are empty on every row — UNVERIFIED (F-01) — and are shown so the shape of the finished product is visible and so the gap is unmistakable.

The origination order is fixed: side, then total, then moneyline

#MarketHow it is producedSource of the inputs
1sideridge margin model: home_field + net[home] - net[away]; published as the home spreadsource
2totalridge pace model: base + pace[home] + pace[away]source
3moneylinederived from the side (step 1) and the walk-forward margin sigma via the normal CDF; converted to American oddssource

The moneyline is deliberately last because it is derived: it needs the side (step 1) and the walk-forward margin sigma. Reversing the order would mean pricing a probability before pricing the margin it comes from.
