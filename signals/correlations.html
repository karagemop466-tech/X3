How to read this

  • n is the sample, not the season. Game-level pairs use the 137 verified finals; model pairs use only the 105 walk-forward graded games; club pairs use 32 clubs; availability pairs use 30 games because the official injury capture began at week 8.
  • A club-level n of 32 is small. The Pythagorean and point-differential correlations clear their intervals easily; the scoring-versus-conceding one does not, and is printed as a null.
  • The availability rows are the interesting null. The offensive absence load differential correlates with the home margin at roughly r = 0.36 on n = 30 with an interval that just includes zero. That is a reason to get more weeks of injury data, not a reason to trade it.
