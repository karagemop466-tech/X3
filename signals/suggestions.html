Suggestions — choose a path before changing anything

Nothing on this page has been implemented. Every item is a proposal with the paths laid out, the trade-offs written down and the decision left open. That is the point: the value of a suggestion register is that a decision gets made on the record before the work starts, not afterwards.

Each row states the problem it solves, the paths available, what each path costs or risks, what it unlocks, and a value/effort read. The decision and decided by columns are empty on every row and stay empty until someone fills them in.

S-01Price captureLicence or key in an official-or-better price series
Nothing on this site can compute ROI, closing-line value or staking performance, because no price exists. Every payoff column is empty.
A: buy a commercial odds archive. B: key the opening and closing numbers in weekly from one sportsbook at a fixed hour. C: do nothing and keep the leaderboard price-free.
A is accurate and auditable but costs money. B is free and reproducible but depends on one person's discipline. C costs nothing and caps what the project can ever prove.Unlocks ROI, CLV, drawdown, Kelly staking, the demotion gates D3 and partly D1, and a real leaderboard.highmediumproposed — not implemented——source
S-02Data captureAdd an official per-game player-statistics capture to CI
The four player-prop-combo strategies (R1-R4) cannot be scored at all: the repository holds no per-game player numbers.
A: capture nfl.com player game logs. B: capture nfl.com weekly team/player stat leaders only. C: drop prop strategies from scope.
A is the only route to genuine prop markets. B is cheap but covers a handful of players. C removes a whole strategy family the brief asks for.Unlocks the prop-combo family and the injury-to-target-share link.highhighproposed — not implemented——source
S-03Data qualityCapture the as-of date on each club depth chart
0 of the captured charts carry an as-of date and most are labelled unofficial by the club, so the depth layer cannot be audited.
A: scrape the chart page's own updated-on text. B: use the NFL's official weekly transaction report as the clock instead. C: leave undated and keep the layer advisory.
A depends on each club's page markup. B is uniform across clubs and comes from the league. C is honest but weak evidence.Makes the 2nd/3rd/4th-string layer auditable rather than indicative.mediumlowproposed — not implemented——source
S-04Data captureBackfill injury reports for weeks 1-7
The availability layer covers 3 of 10 played weeks, which is why no availability coefficient is fitted.
A: backfill from the same official injury pages. B: start fresh next season. C: keep the 3-week window.
A triples the sample immediately. B delays the payoff a year. C leaves the requirement only partly served.Triples the availability sample and is the precondition for S-08.highlowproposed — not implemented——source
S-05GovernanceDecide the sample-size gate: keep 200 or lower it
At 137 finals nothing can clear a 200-decision gate, so the leaderboard is empty by construction.
A: keep 200 and wait. B: lower to 100 with a wider interval. C: publish a provisional tier below the gate, labelled as such.
A is the most rigorous and the slowest. B raises the false-positive rate. C gives the leaderboard something to show without claiming skill.Determines when the leaderboard can ever be non-empty.highlowproposed — not implemented——source
S-06ModelPublish an interval on the home-field constant
The constant is refitted weekly and printed as a bare number, so a reader cannot tell movement from noise.
A: bootstrap interval. B: report the week-by-week series and let the reader see the spread. C: fix it at a conventional value.
A is the most informative. B is cheap and honest. C throws away a measured quantity.Makes the home-field strategy S8 scoreable.mediumlowproposed — not implemented——source
S-07ModelReplace the normal margin model with the empirical distribution
NFL margins cluster on 3 and 7, so a normal CDF misprices the moneyline near those numbers.
A: empirical CDF from verified finals. B: a discrete key-number model. C: keep the normal approximation and flag it.
A is exact but needs a large sample. B is the industry-standard fix. C is the cheapest and least accurate.Improves moneyline calibration, which feeds the Brier skill score.mediummediumproposed — not implemented——source
S-08ModelFit the availability coefficient walk-forward once the injury weeks are backfilled
The injury-and-depth layer is published but never enters a line, because its coefficient is not identified on 3 weeks.
A: fit walk-forward with a ridge penalty. B: fit per unit (offense, defense). C: leave the layer advisory forever.
A is the standard approach. B is more expressive but needs more data. C keeps the layer honest but inert.Turns the depth tracker into a model input instead of a report.mediummediumproposed — not implemented——source
S-09RewardsDecide the leaderboard reward structure
Rewards are notional. Paying on an unvalidated signal funds luck.
A: notional only until a strategy clears every blocking gate. B: small fixed monthly prize pool. C: tiered, paid only on signal score.
A is the safe default. B pays for participation. C rewards the thing we actually want but needs a validated signal first.Makes the competition real without paying for noise.lowlowproposed — not implemented——source
S-10ModelHandle neutral-site games explicitly
The model applies the same home-field constant to neutral-site games, where there is no home field.
A: fit a separate neutral-site constant. B: zero the home field for those games. C: exclude them from grading.
A needs a big neutral-site sample. B is a defensible prior. C loses real games from the record.Removes a known bias on the international and neutral-site slate.lowlowproposed — not implemented——source
S-11VerificationWire every module's verifier into one CI job
Different modules have different verifiers and pages.yml does not run all of them, so a regression can pass CI.
A: one job that runs every verifier. B: one workflow per module. C: leave as is.
A is the only way to guarantee a single definition of green. B is modular but the union is never checked. C is the status quo.Makes 'the site builds with no broken links' a checked statement rather than an asserted one.mediumlowproposed — not implemented——source

How to use this page

  • Pick the item. Cross-reference the question it answers on the questions page.
  • Choose a path — including "do nothing", which is a real option and is listed where it applies.
  • Record the decision and who made it. Until those two cells are filled the item is not approved.
  • Only then implement. An unrecorded decision is invisible to the next person reading this repository.

Suggested order if you only do three

  1. S-01, price capture — it unblocks ROI, closing-line value, staking, drawdown and half the demotion gates at once.
  2. S-04, backfill injury weeks — cheap, and it triples the sample behind the depth requirement.
  3. S-12, retire the overlapping site trees — thirteen trees give thirteen answers to the same question; that is a decision-making problem before it is a code problem.
