Price-snapshot store

Every market this module originates is written twice: an opening number, generated with the ratings fitted through week W−2, and a closing number, generated at the decision cutoff with the ratings refitted through week W−1. Both are stored. 738 of 978 rows carry a number; the rest are recorded as not originated because there were fewer than 24 training finals.

What "opening" and "closing" mean here. They are timestamps on our own information set, not market prices. The opening snapshot is what we would have published when the board first went up; the closing snapshot is what we would publish at kickoff. The gap between them — the movement column — is how much one extra week of results moved our number.

UNVERIFIED (F-01) The market columns are empty. The NFL does not publish betting lines or odds, so there is no official source for a market number or for the difference between our number and the market's. Both columns exist, are printed on the lines page, and are empty on every row. They are not estimated, interpolated or filled in.

The ridge penalty is chosen, not assumed

For each week the ridge penalty is selected by nested walk-forward validation inside the training window only: fit on weeks up to T−1, score on week T, and take the penalty with the lowest validation error. Nothing from the week being originated touches that choice, so the penalty is not tuned on the data it is graded against. The chosen value is stored on every row so the store is auditable.
