Injuries and depth of the replacement

The requirement is not to record that a starter is out — it is to record how deep the replacement is. A starting left tackle replaced by the second string is a different event from one replaced by the fourth. Both the absent player's depth string and the replacement's depth string are published here, per absence.

Three honest limits on this layer. UNVERIFIED (F-05) Coverage is weeks 8, 9 and 10 only — the capture began at week 8, so 7 of the 10 played weeks have no injury data at all. UNVERIFIED (F-03) Zero of the club depth charts carry a machine-readable as-of date and most are labelled unofficial by the club itself. UNVERIFIED (F-09) The weights used to turn an absence into a load number are pre-registered design choices, not measurements; they are printed on the sources page so a reviewer can change them. No published line on this site is adjusted by this layer, because three weeks of coverage cannot identify a coefficient.

Per club, per week

Availability load sums status weight × depth weight × replacement-depth weight over that club's absences. Absences whose depth string could not be resolved are excluded from the load and counted in the "depth resolved" column, so a blank reads as a capture gap rather than a zero.
