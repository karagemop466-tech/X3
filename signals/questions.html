Open questions

A decision made on this site is only as good as the answers behind it. This page separates what we already hold from what we do not, and says how to go and get the missing piece. Where the answer is not knowable from the data in this repository, it says so instead of guessing.

IDTopicQuestionWhat we have What is missingFlagHow to find outSuggestionRepository
Q01Closing lineWhat is the market's closing number for each game?We have none.The NFL does not publish betting lines. Its official sportsbook partners (Caesars, DraftKings, FanDuel) and its data distributor (Genius Sports) do, but none of them is an open official feed this repository can cite per row.UNVERIFIED (F-01)Licence a price feed or key one in per week; until then every market-line cell stays empty.S-01source
Q02Opening lineWhat is the market's opening number?We have none.Same source gap as Q01, and it is the more damaging one: closing-line value — the standard skill test — cannot be computed without it.UNVERIFIED (F-01)S-01 covers this too. Capture the opening number at a fixed weekday and hour so the snapshot is reproducible.S-01source
Q03Payout / settlementWhat did a winning bet actually pay?We have none.No price means no payout, so ROI, drawdown and Kelly staking cannot be evaluated at all.UNVERIFIED (F-02)S-01 unlocks this automatically once prices exist.S-01source
Q04Player propsWhat are the official per-game player statistics?We have none captured.nfl.com publishes player and team statistics, but the capture in this repository covers week schedule pages, standings, injury reports and club depth charts only.UNVERIFIED (F-04)Add an official player-stats capture to the CI workflow (S-02).S-02source
Q05Depth chart as-of dateWhen was each club depth chart last updated?We have none.0 of the captured club charts carry a machine-readable as-of date, and most are labelled unofficial by the club itself.UNVERIFIED (F-03)Capture the chart page's own updated-on text, or fall back to the NFL's official weekly transaction report (S-03).S-03source
Q06Injury coverage windowDo we have injury reports for weeks 1-7?No — weeks 8, 9 and 10 only.The capture began at week 8, so the availability layer covers 3 of 10 played weeks.UNVERIFIED (F-05)Backfill weeks 1-7 from the same official injury pages (S-04).S-04source
Q07Sample sizeIs 137 finals enough to claim skill?No.The pre-registered gate is 200 graded decisions. The largest graded set here is well under it, and the selective subsets are smaller still.UNVERIFIED (F-06)Wait for more weeks, or lower the gate — lowering it is a decision, not a finding (S-05).S-05source
Q08Home-field constantIs the fitted home-field constant stable?It is refitted every week and printed on the lines page.A constant that moves a lot week to week is a sign the model is under-identified, not a finding about home field.—Report it with an interval once the sample grows (S-06).S-06source
Q09Margin distributionIs the normal approximation for the moneyline safe?It is an assumption, not a measurement.NFL margins are not normal — they cluster on 3 and 7 — so the moneyline probability is approximate.UNVERIFIED (F-07)Replace the normal CDF with the empirical distribution of historical margins (S-07).S-07source
Q10Availability coefficientHow much does an absence move the line?Not identified.Three weeks of coverage is far too little to fit a coefficient, so no availability adjustment is applied to any published number.UNVERIFIED (F-05)Backfill the injury weeks first (S-04), then fit the coefficient walk-forward (S-08).S-08source
Q11RewardsWhat does the leaderboard actually pay?Nothing yet.Rewards are notional. Paying real money on an unvalidated signal would fund luck, not skill.—Decide the reward structure only after a strategy clears every blocking gate (S-09).S-09source
Q12Neutral-site gamesAre neutral-site games handled?They are flagged, not adjusted.The results feed flags them, but the model applies the same home-field constant to every game.UNVERIFIED (F-08)Fit a separate neutral-site constant, or drop those games from grading (S-10).S-10source

The three that block everything else

  1. Q01 — closing line. Without the market's closing number there is no closing-line value, and without closing-line value the standard test for skill in this field is simply unavailable.
  2. Q04 — player statistics. Four of the fifteen strategies are unscoreable until official per-game player numbers exist.
  3. Q07 — sample size. Even a perfect model cannot clear a 200-decision gate on 105 graded games. This is a decision to make, not a problem to solve.
