The headline

We can originate a side that beats a constant baseline, and we cannot yet originate a total that does. Over 105 walk-forward graded games the side was closer to the actual margin than a no-knowledge baseline in 67 of 105 (0.638, 95% CI 0.543–0.724, exact binomial p = 0.0060). The total was closer in 47 of 105 (0.448, p = 0.3291) — and on the games where the total model was most confident it was wrong 21 times out of 29 (p = 0.0241). That is a significant adverse result.

None of this is profit. This repository holds no market price, so no bet can be priced, no ROI computed and no staking rule evaluated. Everything below is a measurement of forecast accuracy against a constant, not a claim that money would have been made.

What we recommend, in order

  1. Do not stake anything. Nothing is live, so nothing can be demoted — but the point of the demotion gate is that nothing should be promoted yet either.
  2. Get a price series. S-01. Until a market number exists for each originated market, closing-line value — the one test that separates skill from a good run — is impossible.
  3. Fix the total before adding anything else. The total model is not merely uninformative, it is adverse where it is confident. Adding strategies on top of it multiplies the problem.
  4. Backfill the missing weeks of injury data. S-04. Three weeks of coverage is why the depth layer is a report and not an input.
  5. Capture official player statistics. S-02. Without them the four player-prop-combo strategies cannot be scored at all.

How to read every table on this site

  • A value is printed only if it was computed from data in this repository.
  • An empty cell is a dash, and the row carries an UNVERIFIED badge with a flag id.
  • Every other row carries a link to the official page the numbers came from.
  • Every correlation carries its n, its 95% interval and its p-value. No n, no claim.
