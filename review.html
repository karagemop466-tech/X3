Decision queue

Use this page to review paths before implementation. Priority reflects dependency and decision value, not a factual claim. Each recommendation states what evidence to collect and what remains unanswered.

Questions that must be answered before a reasonable decision

Goal & constraints

What does “useful” mean—forecast accuracy, positive expected value, risk-adjusted return, or decision support?

Also define jurisdiction, permitted markets, bankroll assumptions (if applicable), risk limits, timeline, and whether outputs are private research or public.

Unknown · owner decision required

Evidence & data rights

What authoritative historical odds and timestamps can be lawfully accessed? How complete are injury report revisions and official player outcomes?

Official NFL pages linked on the data page do not verify bookmaker prices, provider licensing, or complete historical availability.

Unknown · source verification

Strategy evaluation

Which season range, markets, decision cutoff, settlement rules, baseline, holdout and uncertainty standard should be pre-registered?

Until chosen, a leaderboard threshold or claimed skill edge would be arbitrary.

Unknown · protocol

Correlation & parlay prices

Can historical joint-leg outcomes and actual offered parlay payouts be captured at the same time?

Without these, dependence and expected value for player-prop combinations cannot be estimated; multiplying leg probabilities assumes independence and is not acceptable.

Unknown · blocked

Lineup deficiencies

What evidence defines starter, backup rank, activation, actual snaps, and a measurable defensive/offensive deficiency?

Rank labels vary and an injury alone does not prove replacement impact. Require dated team/league records and test effect against a baseline.

Unknown · measure, don't infer

Leaderboard rewards

What reward, eligibility, period, scoring, minimum sample, tie-break, audit and responsible-gambling terms are acceptable?

Rewards should not be set until test protocol and uncertainty-aware score are fixed.
