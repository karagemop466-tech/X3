Strategy

Leaderboard Competition

A competition between strategies, scored on risk-adjusted out-of-sample performance against the platform's own originated lines. Rewards go to the strategies that clear every validation gate, not to the ones that got lucky.

A competition between strategies, scored on risk-adjusted out-of-sample performance against the platform's own originated lines. Rewards go to the strategies that clear every validation gate, not to the ones that got lucky.

Read this before the standings

The platform originates its own lines. A strategy is therefore scored on whether it disagrees with the line model profitably, using signals that are deliberately different from the line model's own inputs. This is stated up front because a strategy evaluated against a reference it was fitted to would measure self-consistency rather than skill. See suggestion S-002 for the stronger design that is still open.

Scoring

ComponentDefinitionWeightWhy
Risk-adjusted returnNet units divided by total units risked, divided by the standard deviation of per-bet units.35%Rewards efficiency of return rather than size of return, so that a strategy cannot win by over-sizing.
Drawdown controlThe negative of maximum peak-to-trough drawdown, measured as a fraction of the units staked.20%A strategy that survives is worth more than one that returns more before failing.
Out-of-sample sign consistencyThe share of evaluation folds in which the strategy's edge was positive, and the sign consistency across seasons.20%Consistency across independent periods is the clearest observable difference between skill and luck.
Skill shareThe share of observed outcome variance explained by the model's own probability, with its bootstrap interval.25%This is the component that operationalises the brief's instruction to remove luck from the equation.
Normalisation
Each component is converted to a z-score across the eligible strategies for the cycle, then weighted and summed. The composite is then converted back to a readable scale for display.
Tie-breaks
Higher skill share. · Lower maximum drawdown. · Earlier registration in the hypothesis register. · If still tied, the strategies are declared joint winners and the reward for that tier is split.
Profit excluded
Raw profit is deliberately excluded from the composite. A strategy is not rewarded for being large, only for being good.

Per-class awards
Each strategy class (single, parlay, prop, prop combo) is also scored on its own, with a class award, so that a strong prop strategy is not permanently outranked by a strong spread strategy.
Pool size
Not yet set. This is open question Q-013. Until the pool is published, the leaderboard is a scoreboard and not a funded competition.
Transparency rule
The reward schedule, the gate thresholds and the scoring weights are published before a cycle opens and are not changed during a cycle.

Eligibility and fair play

Eligibility rule
A strategy is eligible for scoring and reward only if it clears every gate G1 to G10. A failing gate is displayed with its measured value so the failure is legible, and the strategy is excluded from the reward pool.
Gate table
Published on the Strategies page with the measured value for each strategy.
Ineligible display
Ineligible strategies remain visible on the leaderboard, clearly marked, so that the competition cannot look selective.
Hypothesis register
Every hypothesis tested in a cycle is registered, whether it passed or failed, and the register is published at the cycle close. A cycle in which many hypotheses were tried and only one is shown is treated as a failed disclosure and is not scored.
Multiple testing
A Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate control is applied across the register at the 10 per cent level, as gate G3.
No reuse
The same evaluation window may not be used to select a strategy and then to score it. The scoring window must follow the selection window.
Price independence
A strategy may not use a commercial closing line, a projection service or any non-official feed. Every input must appear in the source manifest with an official URL.
