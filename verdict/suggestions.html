Recommended order of work

  1. Make result truth deterministic. Refresh both result feeds from official game centers, preserve raw source and retrieval time, and fail on score/coverage mismatch. Reconcile 150 finals after Week 10 before any backtest.
  2. Choose price policy before evaluation. Name permitted books/feeds, opening/closing definitions, timestamps, push/void settlement, and retention/terms. Until then the market/residual columns remain empty.
  3. Build a point-in-time ledger before personnel modeling. Immutable official injury reports, club depth pages, inactive lists, Game Books, transactions and official snap counts; preserve hashes, timestamps, conflicts and source strings.
  4. Start singles-only in shadow. Pre-register probabilities and inputs before kickoff, benchmark against favorite-only and seeded random controls, evaluate with chronological holdouts and proper scores plus uncertainty.
  5. Keep combo strategies out of ranking until joint outcomes are validated. Marginal probabilities cannot establish SGP/parlay/prop-combo probabilities under dependence; document settlement and void rules.
  6. Fix the decision-page deploy gate in its owning scope. Request changes from the decision-layer owner; do not regenerate their pages from this branch.
