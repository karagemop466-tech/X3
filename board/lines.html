Why the difference column is empty rather than estimated. A residual is fair − market. With no market number, any residual would be the fair number wearing a disguise. The rule the repository adopted is printed on data/closeout/derived/price_policy.csv: the fair line is a number, the difference is a formula, and it becomes live the moment an admitted price source exists. Until then this is a board of opinions with no score.

2. What the fair lines are made of

The model is a walk-forward net-points rating with a fitted home advantage. Every ingredient is printed on the row: team rating, home advantage, the residual standard deviation used for the probabilities, and the number of games the ratings were trained on. Two properties matter for the honest reading:

  • Home advantage is fitted, not assumed. This season's league mean home margin is −0.53 points, so the model carries −0.157. That is unusual and it is in the data, not imposed on it.
  • The ratings are not opponent-aware. They are net points per game, which rewards a club for beating a weak schedule. Making them opponent-aware is the brief's requirement, and it is not done: there is no game-level opponent-adjusted rating in this repository that is not in-sample fitted.

3. The test I ran, and the result that matters

I ran a rating model with no market input over the 150 settled games, warm-up 3 games per club, re-predicting every game from the games strictly before it. Two variants, identical except for the home-field constant:

Finding. Importing the conventional 2.5-point home advantage cost 0.42 points of mean absolute error and two points of hit rate on this season. The fitted advantage is near zero because the league's actual home margin is negative. Do not hard-code a home edge; fit it from the season, and re-check it every week. The repository's own model already does this correctly, which is the one piece of good news here.

Side hit rate on the no-constant variant: 0.667, game-cluster bootstrap 95% CI [0.559, 0.735] over 102 graded games. This is a directional skill estimate against no line. It is not evidence of profit: beating a market requires beating a number that already contains the public's information, and no such number exists here to beat.

4. Why none of this is a strategy yet

  • No price → no return → no ranking. A model can be accurate and still lose money; the market sets the price at which accuracy becomes profit.
  • 102 graded games with a warm-up is a small sample. Published with its interval, not as a result.
  • The 13 live rows have never been graded. They are a pre-registration: frozen before kickoff, to be scored from the official results after Week 11. That is the only honest use for them today.
  • The model carries no injury, personnel or availability input at all — every Week-11 row records availability_input = NONE because no Week-11 report was published at freeze time. That is a stated limitation, not a null feature.
