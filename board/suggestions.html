Suggestions for review before any change is made

Twelve proposals, ordered by what they unblock. Each says what to do, why it matters, what it requires, and who decides. Nothing here is implemented on this page. It is a review queue: pick a path, then implement it.

How to choose. SG-01 costs nothing and fixes a live defect. SG-02 is the one decision that unblocks the most, and it is not a technical one. SG-04 through SG-09 are sequenced: each makes the next cheaper. SG-10 through SG-12 are hygiene.

If you only do three things

  1. SG-01. Repoint the results feed. It takes an afternoon, needs no new data, and removes the single most misleading number on the site.
  2. SG-02. Decide the price question. Until it is answered, every strategy metric on this board is decorative, and no amount of modelling changes that.
  3. SG-04. Turn on the capture cadence. Every week that passes without a dated injury read is a week that cannot be backtested later, and it is the one thing that cannot be recovered retroactively.

The reasoning behind SG-06 (do not hard-code home advantage) is worked out on Lines & value; it is the only suggestion here backed by a measurement made for this page rather than by a defect observed.

How to read this board.
