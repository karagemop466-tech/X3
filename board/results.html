Results, reconciliation and the removal of luck

Two questions decide whether anything else on this board is meaningful: are the game results right, and how much of a team's record is not skill? The first is checked against the official standings, club by club. The second is computed and its limits are printed with it.

1. The results feed reconciles — on one of its two copies

The finding. This repository stores the 2026 season twice. The 150-game log in the close-out tree recomputes to the official standings exactly: all 32 clubs match on wins, losses, ties, points for and points against, and league scoring balances (ΣPF = ΣPA). The 137-game feed in the canonical verified tree still marks 13 of the 14 Week-10 games SCHEDULED — and that is the copy the landing page labels "verified results feed".

Neither file is dishonest about its own contents. The failure is that two answers to "how many games have been played" are both published, and the wrong one is prominent. The fix is to retire one file, not to re-transcribe anything.

2. The removal-of-luck panel

Pythagorean expectation converts points scored and allowed into the record a club's scoring would normally produce. The gap between that and the actual record is the part of the record the scoring does not explain. Formula: pythag = PF^2.37 / (PF^2.37 + PA^2.37); expected_wins = pythag × games; luck_delta = actual_wins − expected_wins, with a tie counted as half a win. The exponent 2.37 is the conventional value and is not fitted here — fitting it would tune the panel to the answer.

All 32 rows computed from the official standings read 2026-11-18T03:20Z. Games are 9 or 10, so a delta of one win is within a single game's swing: read the sign, not the decimals.

What this panel is for

A leaderboard that ranks strategies on realised return will, over a short run, mostly rank this column. The clubs at the top of the table above are not the best teams; they are the teams whose scoring has been least predictive of their record. Any strategy that looks profitable because it backed them is measuring the same noise. That is why staking is separated from signal on Strategies, and why no strategy is ranked until the market price needed to compute a return exists.

What the panel cannot tell you

  • It cannot separate luck from a genuine ability to win close games. Both produce the same delta. Saying which one it is needs a multi-season sample, and the repository holds one season.
  • It is not a forecast. A club with a positive delta is not "due to regress" by arithmetic; the claim that it will is a hypothesis that needs testing on out-of-sample seasons.
  • The exponent is a convention. A different exponent moves every row.

How to read this board. Every number is either (a) transcribed from an official NFL page whose link is on the row, (b) arithmetic on those numbers with the formula printed beside it, or (c) marked BLOCKED / UNVERIFIED with the missing input named. Nothing is estimated, interpolated or inferred. Where two official pages disagree, both are linked and the row is flagged on Flagged for review.
