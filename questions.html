Q-001 Is this platform for private analysis of games the owner bets on, or is it intended to publish lines that other people bet against (i.e. operate a book)? Scope & Legal blocking

The two answers produce completely different platforms. Publishing a line with a stake attached is a regulated activity in most jurisdictions; analysing one's own bets is not. Every downstream design decision - prize structure, 'rewards', public leaderboard - depends on this.

Blocks
Reward design, whether the leaderboard is public or private, whether a wagering interface may ever be added, and the entire compliance workstream.
Owner
Platform owner
Status
open
What we already know
  • The NFL publishes its official playing rules and rule changes through NFL Football Operations. (NFL Football Operations - 2026 NFL Rulebook)
  • The NFL publishes official league statements, including policy positions, through NFL Communications. (NFL Communications (NFL.com))
What is missing
  • Whether the platform will accept, hold or publish stakes.
  • Whether the leaderboard rewards real money, a points-based allowance, or recognition only.
  • Whether lines are made available to any third party.
Suggested actions to discover the missing information
  • Answer the question in one sentence to the platform owner.
  • If the answer is 'publish lines to others', commission a jurisdiction-specific gaming-licensing review before any line is published.
  • If the answer is 'private analysis', record that decision in this register so the leaderboard reward design can be settled.

Q-005 Is per-game player-level data (box score / game book) required, and is it acceptable to source it only from NFL.com game pages? Data blocking

Player-prop strategies and any player-prop combination cannot be evaluated at all without game-level player logs, and the correlation matrix for prop combos is computed from them.

Blocks
Whether the player-prop and prop-combination strategy classes can ever pass validation.
Owner
Platform owner (field list) / data engineering (coverage)
Status
open
What we already know
  • NFL.com publishes the official player-statistics tables by category and season. (NFL.com player statistics by category)
  • NFL.com publishes the official weekly schedule and results, which is the game-level index used to build box-score URLs. (NFL.com weekly schedule)
What is missing

Suggested actions to discover the missing information
  • Run the box-score fetcher over one completed season and measure coverage against the official schedule.
  • Confirm the per-game field set required for each prop market the owner wants to trade.
Q-006 How deep should official depth-chart ingestion go, and who is responsible for keeping it correct? Data non-blocking

The requirement to know when a second, third or fourth string replaces a starter is only satisfiable if the depth ordering is sourced, not inferred. Club depth charts are maintained by each club and are frequently stale or formatted differently.

Blocks
The precision of the deficiency signal and therefore the size of the injury-related edge.
Owner
Data engineering (with owner approval of the fallback rule)
Status
open
What we already know
  • Official club websites publish their own depth charts; the pipeline probes all 32 official club domains and keeps only pages that resolve. (NFL.com team index)
  • The official NFL injury report lists, per team, the players reported for each game and their practice and game status. (NFL.com official injury report)
What is missing
  • Which clubs publish a machine-readable depth chart and which do not.
  • Whether a human curator will correct the gaps, and at what cadence.
  • Whether the official game book 'substitutions' order should be used as the fallback depth signal.
Suggested actions to discover the missing information
  • Run the depth-chart coverage check across all 32 clubs and publish the coverage table.
  • Agree a fallback order: official club depth chart, then official game-book starters and substitutions, then blank-and-flag.

Q-009 How is skill separated from luck, quantitatively, and what threshold must be cleared? Model blocking

This is the core of the brief. A strategy that is lucky looks identical to a skilled strategy on a win/loss record; only variance decomposition and out-of-sample consistency separate them.

Blocks
Eligibility for rewards; the ranking order of the leaderboard.
Owner
Platform owner (with quant review)
Status
open
What we already know

Nothing in this area has been confirmed from an official source yet. The platform has not assumed a value.

What is missing
  • The agreed skill-share threshold and the bootstrap interval width that satisfies it.
  • Whether the skill metric is computed per strategy in isolation or against a portfolio of all deployed strategies.
Suggested actions to discover the missing information

Q-010 May the evaluation reference an independent market consensus, or must every strategy be judged only against our own lines? Model blocking

Judging a strategy against our own line is circular: our line is produced by the same family of models. Without an independent reference the leaderboard can only measure self-consistency, not skill.

Blocks
The validity of the entire leaderboard.
Owner
Platform owner
Status
open
What we already know
  • The platform's own lines are generated from official NFL data; no third-party line feed is ingested, which is the required 'originate our own lines' property. — This repository - scripts/compute_signals.py
What is missing
  • Whether an independent closing-line reference may be consulted for evaluation only (not for line generation).
  • If not, what objective reference replaces it - for example a held-out model trained on a different feature set and period.
Suggested actions to discover the missing information
  • Option A: permit a market reference for evaluation only, with the line-generation path proven independent.
  • Option B: adopt a two-model design where a separately specified evaluator, trained on a disjoint period and feature set, produces the reference probability.
  • Option C: restrict rewards to strategies that beat a null model with a pre-registered significance threshold.
