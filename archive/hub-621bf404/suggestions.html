Recommended path (one paragraph, no code changed beyond the hub)

Start the archive before anything else: prices nightly (S-04) and injury/standings snapshots on the league's own cadence (S-05) - that data cannot be bought back later. Then freeze one data contract and make this hub canonical (S-01, S-03) so two reviewers stop quoting different numbers for one fact. Only after a season of archived history may any parameter be calibrated (S-08, S-09, S-10) - until then the gates stay unpassed by design and the leaderboard stays empty.
