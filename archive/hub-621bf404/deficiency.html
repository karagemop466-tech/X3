Depth and deficiency tracking

Two layers, deliberately kept apart. The certified layer counts who is unavailable from the official report. The string layer - whether a 2nd, 3rd or 4th-quarterback is in - cannot be certified because no official depth chart exists (FLG-08), so those columns are blank by policy. Loss numbers are DERIVED using the uncalibrated weights in the model config.
