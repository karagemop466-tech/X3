Note on the desk column: it uses the same notation as this page (a positive number means the home club is the dog), so it is directly comparable, unlike the dashboard column. Across the four in-repo models the home-spread disagreement for this slate averages 4.6 points and reaches 10.9 points on one game - that spread, not any one column, is the honest picture of an uncalibrated pipeline.

The desk model differs from both others because it is fitted rather than assumed: its home-field term is 0.08 points (estimated by ridge regression on 137 finals) and it applies no injury adjustment at all, so the gap to this page is the injury adjustment plus the parameter choice. Its walk-forward accuracy (MAE 10.52, predicted-vs-actual r 0.2 on 121 games) is the only out-of-sample figure in the repository, and it measures a rating model, not a graded bet.

Exclusions and cautions
