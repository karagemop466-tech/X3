Origination module — overview & executive summary

The honest state of the money question

Backtests are now computable (the results feed exists). ROI is not: no official source publishes NFL market prices, so fair-vs-market differences, CLV and ROI stay blank and flagged rather than invented. The moment a price source is connected, every column the store and the leaderboard reserve for it activates — no schema change needed.
