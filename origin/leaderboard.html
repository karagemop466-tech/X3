Competition rules (registered)

  • The leaderboard is ranked by the SIGNAL column ONLY. Staking columns are displayed next to it but are never blended into the rank, so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal (and vice versa).
  • A strategy needs n >= 100 settled walk-forward signals to hold a rank at all (floor registered before any Week-10 result exists); below it the strategy is UNRANKED, not zero.
  • Signal metrics are computed walk-forward on official finals only; n is published with every value.
  • A strategy enters the competition only after passing gates G-0..G-6 (hub gates) and stays there only while no demotion trigger (origin gates D-1) has fired.
  • Staking columns activate when a market-price source exists (open decision Q-01); until then every staking cell stays blank and BLOCKED.
  • Rewards: the brief provides for rewards for top-performing strategies, but the reward pool, currency and funding are an owner decision (question Q-A06) - nothing is promised or published here.

Leg-dependence for the 2-leg parlay: phi = see backtest — phi is published with its n there and flagged as a tiny sample (F-A06).
