Gates & demotion

Validation gates for the origination module, plus the demotion gate D-1 (requirement R8): a live strategy is sent back to paper by mechanical triggers whose thresholds are registered NOW — before any strategy is live — so the gate cannot be argued with after the fact. The hub's gates G-0..G-6 remain the base layer (hub/gates.html); this page registers the module's own evidence and the D-1/D-2 lifecycle.

Demotion gate (live -> paper) — REGISTERED(no strategy is live; triggers armed at promotion)

Any single trigger moves the strategy from live to paper for at least the next full cycle. Open positions are closed at the next cycle boundary. The strategy keeps its leaderboard signal row (signal history is never erased) but is marked DEMOTED and cannot be staked until re-promoted via D-2.

Re-promotion gate (paper -> live)

After a demotion: >= 30 paper-settled signals at the same rules, median CLV >= 0 on those signals once prices exist, G-2/G-4 re-passed on the fresh sample, and no D-1 trigger active for two full cycles. State: REGISTERED
