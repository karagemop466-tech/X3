Walk-forward backtest

Requirement R2 delivered: with the verified results feed in place, backtests are computable. Method (registered before any Week-10 result exists): ridge least squares (λ=1) on margin = home-field + net[home] − net[away] and total = mean + pace[home] + pace[away], fitted on weeks < w and scored on week w finals (walk-forward, no leakage, no randomness). ROI is not published here: no market prices exist, so any ROI number would be invented (flagged F-A01). What the feed enables today is signal evaluation; what it cannot yet do is money-weighted evaluation.
