Mandatory validation gates

GatePass requirementFail / flag rule
1 · Provenance & coverageEvery feature has source, timestamp, definition, coverage and license/access record.Unknown provenance, missing target or unmeasured coverage → block.
2 · Pre-registrationFreeze market, cutoff, features, exclusions, baseline, metric and stake policy before test.Post-hoc changes require a new version and untouched holdout.
3 · Leakage & temporal splitFeatures exist by decision time; use chronological training, validation and untouched test.Any future information or random-only split → fail.
4 · Baseline & priceCompare with calibrated market-implied and simple historical baselines using executable prices net of vig/fees.Model-only accuracy or hypothetical best price → fail.
5 · Calibration & uncertaintyReport log loss/Brier, calibration, confidence intervals, sample size and all tried variants.Win rate alone, cherry-picking, or unclear uncertainty → fail.
6 · RobustnessWalk-forward season/time evaluation, subgroup stability, sensitivity, and independent reproduction.Edge isolated to one split/selection → hold for review.
7 · Correlation & riskEstimate joint dependence on out-of-sample residuals/outcomes; disclose effective exposure and drawdown.Assumed independence or unmeasured multi-leg risk → block parlays.
Leaderboard design: rank only eligible, versioned strategies using net return and uncertainty together; publish sample size, calibration, maximum drawdown, test window and reproducibility. No leaderboard score can remove randomness or guarantee future performance. Minimum evidence thresholds must be chosen and preregistered before any ranking; the repository has no data to set defensible thresholds.
