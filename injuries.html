How the system tracks deficiencies (design)

  • Ingest the official injury report (player, practice status, game status) each week.
  • Ingest the official team depth chart (starter → 4th string) for every position group, offense and defense.
  • Reconcile both at game-day inactives; flag conflicts for review.
  • Produce a per-team missing-starter impact score for offense and defense, quantifying the expected drop from a 2nd/3rd/4th-string replacement.
  • Feed the impact score into signals and the leaderboard.
