Overview

Executive summary

The data and governance layers are live and verified. The betting layer is built but cannot fire until market lines and a game log exist.

  • Repository — The repo contained exactly one file, README.md, four bytes, one commit. There was no prior code, data, or documentation to review. Full audit on the Repo Review page.
  • Verified data — All 32 club records from the official 2026 league standings, the official Week 10 schedule, and 294 rows of the official Week 10 injury report were read from NFL.com and transcribed row by row. official source ↗
  • Depth coverage — 31 of 32 official club depth charts were read. Houston publishes its chart only as an image, so it is recorded as a gap, not filled in. official source ↗
  • The depth charts are not official — Every club labels its own chart 'unofficial'. 27 of 32 print no date. Two clubs contradict their own news pages. This is the single biggest data-quality finding.
  • Injury and depth disagree — Detroit lists Cade Mays as its starting centre while the official injury report lists him Out. The pipeline therefore treats the injury report as authoritative for availability and the chart only for replacement order. official source ↗
  • Own lines — A rating-derived spread is produced for every Week 10 game. It runs with home-field advantage at 0.0 and with no schedule-strength adjustment, because both require a game log that has not been ingested. Every model output is badged uncalibrated.
  • Strategies — Ten strategies are registered across single bets, parlays and prop combos. Four can fire on today's data. Six are blocked pending play-by-play or a correlation matrix.
  • Gates — Twelve validation gates. All twelve are blocking. Nothing can stake until it has cleared all of them out-of-sample.
  • Correlation — Four correlations were computed from the verified standings. Eight leg-level and strategy-level correlations are deliberately left blank because the required dataset does not exist yet.
  • Blocking unknowns — Two things stop the betting layer from firing at all: no market lines (Q-02) and no game log (Q-01). Both are answerable, but the market-line answer needs an owner decision because no official source publishes odds.
  • Decisions waiting — Sixteen suggestions across three statuses: two adopted, thirteen proposed, one deferred. Each has named options and a recommendation so a reviewer can pick a path before anything ships.
  • Legal — The owner is in Tokyo. Whether originating lines and staking real money is lawful there is unresolved. Recommend notional stakes until it is answered.
