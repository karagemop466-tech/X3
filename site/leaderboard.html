Anti-gaming rules

  • Pre-registration (G-01) means a strategy cannot be rewritten after it has results.
  • The minimum-sample gate (G-02) makes a short hot streak worthless.
  • The concentration gate (G-10) stops a strategy that is really one lucky week.
  • The parameter-sensitivity gate (G-08) stops a strategy fitted to noise.
  • The capacity gate stops a strategy that only works at a stake nobody can actually get down.
  • Every graded bet must carry the line and the fetch timestamp that produced it (G-09, G-11).
Recommendation: run the first season on notional stakes (S-12). Cash rewards create an incentive to game the score before we know whether the gates work.
