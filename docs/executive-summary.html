Executive summary

Snapshot as of 2026-11-14 (Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season). This summary states only what is verified.

1. What this project is

A research workspace to collect NFL information, track injuries, and test candidate strategies for NFL lines (single bets, parlays and player-prop combos). Strategies are ranked on a leaderboard only after passing validation gates.

2. What is verified now

  • Week 10 official injury report captured: 205 rows across 28 teams (NFL.com source).
  • Out designations on the report: 66; Doubtful: 10; Questionable: 84.
  • Most Out designations by position: LB (10), DT (8), C (7), TE (7).
  • 2026 season key dates from the NFL schedule announcement: 12 verified items (NFL Media).

3. What is not known yet

  • No strategy is validated. There is no verified historical odds, results or depth-chart data, so ROI, CLV, sample size and correlations are blank.
  • Backup analysis is not built. Starter-to-backup replacement status needs official depth charts (Questions Q05).
  • Injury history is limited. Only one official snapshot (Week 10) has been captured (Questions Q04).

4. Key risks

  • Legal: Wagering with private bookmakers from Japan is a criminal offence for the bettor under Penal Code Article 185 (official source). Legal review is needed before any wagering (Suggestion S08).
  • Source conflicts: The NFL.com page titled 2026 important dates lists 2025-season dates; Thanksgiving matchups conflict across sources (see Flagged for Review).
  • Data quality: Name and position labels differ between the report and the news article; some injured players show blank injury fields.

5. Decisions needed from you

  • Scope: research only, or research plus paper-trading (Suggestion S08; Questions Q01-Q02).
  • Data sources: which odds provider, and approval to collect depth charts and injury history (Questions Q03-Q06; Suggestions S01-S02).
  • Validation thresholds and reward design (Questions Q07-Q08; Suggestions S03-S07).
