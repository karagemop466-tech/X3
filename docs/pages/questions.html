Decision rule

No irreversible decision (prize spending, live signal publication, staking rules) should be made while its blocking questions are OPEN. Partial questions may proceed with their gaps flagged. Answers must arrive with sources — the same standard as the data.

Each open question has a discovery path — see also Suggestion S-02 (discovery sprint) on the Suggestions board.
