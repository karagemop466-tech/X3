What "originate your own lines" means here

Produce your own fair spread, fair total, and fair win probabilities before looking at the market's final price. The gap between your number and the market's is the raw signal. Everything downstream — gates, staking, leaderboard — exists to tell real skill from luck.

Core model

  • Rating layer: margin-of-victory–damped Elo, walk-forward only. Reference parameter set (K=20, HFA≈52 rating points, ⅓ off-season mean reversion) published in the community replication (source); our own HFA/damping must be calibrated on our data — values blank until calibrated.
  • Margin model: rating gap → expected margin → win probability via a logistic/normal margin distribution. The distribution σ is a calibration parameter — blank by policy (UNVERIFIED), to be fit + bootstrapped from our play-by-play.
  • Totals layer: pace × efficiency projection, adjusted for weather/script.
  • De-vigging: power method (solve Σpᵢᵏ = 1) rather than naive normalization.

"Selectively skill based" — the split

Skill: input quality, model specification, timing, selection filters, correlation estimation, staking discipline. Luck: single-game variance, parlay binomial noise, small-sample ROI. The pipeline separates them mechanically: no strategy is labeled skill-based until it clears the gate stack. Everything else is "lucky until proven otherwise."
