Anti-gaming & fairness rules

  1. Timestamped, immutable submissions before the first kickoff the strategy touches.
  2. No editing after kickoff; amendments create new entries and add to declared trials.
  3. Max 25 live entries per participant per week; entries sharing games are grouped for DSR/PBO (same-game betting across entries is not independent evidence).
  4. Public rules, public metric, public leaderboard during play (BDB model).
  5. Post-season audit: every logged price re-verified against an archived consensus; unreconcilable bets are voided for scoring.
  6. Disqualification: look-ahead, falsified trials, price-sourcing violations (Gate G0).

How luck is removed in ranking — worked mechanism

A 20-bet entry at 60% win rate sounds elite — but at −110 prices a no-skill process produces wild ROI swings over 20 bets. The Monte-Carlo null shows the 95th percentile of luck; an entry ranks only if it clears that, and shrinkage holds its displayed score near the pool mean until the sample supports the claim. DSR further deflates entries selected from many tried variants.
